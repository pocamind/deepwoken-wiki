# Terrains

## Description

**Surfaces** are materials used in the game, such as slate, metal, or wood.

Nearly all terrain elements in Deepwoken are destructible, but in order to destroy something, the player must use an ability that has a hidden "terrain interactability"[\[1\]](#cite-note-terrain-interactability-1) stat. When an ability with this tag is used, it must meet or exceed the "elemental intensity" threshold of the object in order for the object to be destroyed, with this threshold scaling on the size and material of the object. The elemental intensity stat scales on the investment of the correlating attribute, with a base value per-attack which is unknown.

## Material Types

### Wood

-   Easier to destroy with [Flamecharm](Flamecharm.md) Mantras.
-   Is quite weak in general.
-   Gives a 1.5x climb height boost for [Felinors](Felinor.md) instead of the 1.25x like other materials.
-   Commonly found in houses.

### Slate

-   Weakest to [Physical](Attunement-less.md), [Thundercall](Thundercall.md), and [Shadowcast](Shadowcast.md) Mantras.
-   Commonly found on paths, and is almost never used as a housing or wall material, with the exception of [Summer Isle](Summer_Isle.md).

### Ice

-   Weakest to [Flamecharm](Flamecharm.md) Mantras, melting instead of breaking. Is resistant to [Shadowcast](Shadowcast.md) Mantras.
-   Very slippery, making the player to continue a portion of their momentum even after they stop moving.
-   Mainly found in [Minityrsa](Minityrsa.md), especially on the frozen lake with the [Rogue Construct](Rogue_Construct.md).
-   Some [Frostdraw](Frostdraw.md) abilities can place ice patches on the floor, with these ice patches retaining the same properties as the ice material.

### Grass

-   Can be lit on fire with [Flamecharm](Flamecharm.md) and [Thundercall](Thundercall.md) Mantras. Anyone who touches burning terrain will have the [Burn](Status_Effects.md#burn) status effect applied to them.
-   Burning grass will be a bright, glowing orange, before color shifting to black as the grass dies out.
-   Useful if you have Talents to benefit you when you're under the effects of fire, seeing as you can light the ground on fire and use that to your advantage.
-   The [Woodland Boots](Woodland_Boots.md) Talent, [Woodland Terrain](Talents.md#woodland-terrain) provides a 10% movement speed bonus when standing on grass.
-   Found literally everywhere.
-   The color of the grass changes depending on the [Season](Seasons.md); green in Spring/Summer, orange in Autumn, and white in Winter.
    -   During [Hallowtide](Hallowtide.md), the grass is always orange.

### Glass

-   Very, very sensitive and easy to break.
-   Found most commonly in building windows.

### Metal

-   Very strong.
-   Requires a lot of elemental intensity to break.
-   Found in many locations, most notably [Miner's Landing](<Miner's_Landing.md>), and [Summer Isle](Summer_Isle.md).

### Sand

-   Commonly found on beaches.

### Fabric

-   Functions the same as wood, except a bit weaker.
-   Similarly to grass, it can be lit on fire with [Flamecharm](Flamecharm.md) and [Thundercall](Thundercall.md) Mantras.

## Trivia

-   The Attunement with the most Mantras that can interact with terrain is [Flamecharm](Flamecharm.md). On the other hand, [Bloodrend](Bloodrend.md), [Ironsing](Ironsing.md), and [Galebreathe](Galebreathe.md) have the fewest Mantras with the Terrain Interactability stat.
-   Some weapons have a critical attack that can destroy terrain.
-   Some objects are naturally destructible, having a set amount of "object health" that any attack can damage, even basic attacks. This is most often seen in destructible walls.
-   Due to Developers often having "dev spec" builds with an unobtainable amount of investment, they can destroy very large and significant portions of the map with abilities like [Strong Left](Attunement-less.md#strong-left).
