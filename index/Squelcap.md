## Description

**Squelcap** is a [Food](Food.md) item and crafting ingredient. It is also obtained from [Simforea](Simforea.md) exclusively.

It is a type of **Generic** food.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 5% Stomach and 5% Water.
