# Fishing

“ The depth of water you fish at affects what you'll catch. Some fish are best caught at the shore, and others far out on a boat! ”

― [Loading Screen](Loading_Screen.md) tip

## Description

**Fishing** is a mechanic in _Deepwoken_ that allows players to catch seafood and other valuables from almost any body of water using a [Fishing Rod](Fishing_Rod.md).

Fishing Rods can be crafted with two [Sticks](Stick.md) and one [Fiber](Fiber.md) at a [Crafting Station](Crafting_Station.md) [CraftingStation](Crafting_Station.md), or purchased from select vendors for 30/35.

To fish, cast a line into any body of water by pressing M1, or hold M1 to cast if farther and higher. Once the bobber lands into water, it will take some to settle before a minigame pops up to fish. Once something is caught onto the line, reel in catches by pressing the displayed key (W, A, S, or D) while holding down M1 before the caught item gets loose. Lines can also be instantly withdrawn by pressing M1 while its being thrown.

Fishing also grants experience toward all _physical_ [Attributes](Attributes.md).

## Catches

Fishing can yield a variety of fish, creatures, and other items. Every possible catch has a unique chat message displayed by the player's character upon being reeled in, with most being fish-related puns or other wordplay.

The longer it takes to successfully reel in a catch, the higher the quality of the fish and potential loot obtained. On rare occasions, a catch may be **golden**. With the sole exception of the [Golden Swordfish](Golden_Swordfish.md), golden catches are automatically converted into 1 [Knowledge](Knowledge.md) instead of being added to the player's inventory, up to a maximum of five times per fish.

## Fishing Spots

The depth and salinity of the water being fished in determine the possible catches. For example, [Sea Bass](Sea_Bass.md) can be found near shore but cannot be caught in deep oceans or rivers.

Certain areas have higher fishing activity, resulting in more frequent nibbles. A select locations also contain designated fishing spots, which provide significantly better loot pools than regular waters. Fishing spots appear as small circular areas with water splashes emerging from them. They can be found naturally throughout the world or summoned by placing a [Rain Spire](Rain_Spire.md).

## Trivia

-   By catching 5 of each golden fish, you can earn up to a maximum of 45 just from fishing.
-   The general format and delivery of the lines that your character speaks is a possible reference to the _Animal Crossing_ series.
-   There is no height limit for fishing, allowing players to throw out their lines from the peaks of mountains.
-   Fishing used to contain every single item inside of its lootpool as long as it was in **any chest pool**, this led to infamous incidents such as the **Verse 1 Enchant Shard incident**. After this happened, a quick patch was rolled out to allow blacklists to items and chest pools, such as the blacklisting of Enchant Stone chests.
-   During the [Warring States Era](Warring_States_Era.md) fishing would give points to the aspect you had.
-   Fishing up a [Golden Swordfish](Golden_Swordfish.md) allows you to use it as a weapon.
-   [Lionfish](Lionfish.md) are able to be caught in the lake in [New Kyrsa](Second_Layer/New_Kyrsa.md), and will still be capable of using their Cero attack.
