## Description

The **Threadcutters** make up a part of the [Lord Regent](The_Lord_Regent.md)'s guards. They are known to use [Palace Tachis](Palace_Tachi.md), swords made of alloys that create slow but devastating cutting power.

## Trivia

-   Given that the Palace Tachi's bears similarities to the [Endless Wave](The_Endless_Wave.md), an ōdachi wielded by the [Shoguns](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>), it is likely that the Threadcutters are a light infantry of the Lord Regent's guards.
