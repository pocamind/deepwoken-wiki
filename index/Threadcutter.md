## Description

The **Threadcutters** make up a part of [The Lord Regent](The_Lord_Regent.md)'s guards. They are known to use [Palace Tachi](Palace_Tachi.md)'s, swords made of alloys that create slow but devastating cutting power.[\[1\]](#cite-note-1)

## Trivia

-   Given that the [Palace Tachi](Palace_Tachi.md)'s bears similarities to [the Endless Wave](The_Endless_Wave.md), an ōdachi wielded by the [Shoguns](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>), it is likely that the Threadcutters are a light infantry of the [Lord Regent](The_Lord_Regent.md)'s guards.
