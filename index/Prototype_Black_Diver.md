**Prototype Black Diver** is a Unique (Spec/Mod) [Outfit](Outfits.md) in _Deepwoken_.

## Statistics

### _Requirements_

This Outfit costs 1,000 and requires no materials to craft.

This Outfit additionally requires the player to be at least [Power](Power.md) 15.

### _Armor Stats_

This Outfit has 2800 durability and grants +80% Physical resistance ResistancePhysical, +60% Elemental resistance [ResistanceElemental](Attunements.md), +80% Ice Armor [ResistanceFrostdraw](Frostdraw.md), +80% Ironsing Armor [ResistanceIronsing](Ironsing.md), and +2% Ether Regen [IconEther](Ether.md).

-   Because related resistances from non-[Equipment](Equipment.md) stat sources are combined multiplicatively, and not additively, the total resistances are: 80% Physical ResistancePhysical, 92% Frostdraw [ResistanceFrostdraw](Frostdraw.md), 92% Ironsing [ResistanceIronsing](Ironsing.md), 60% Elemental [ResistanceBloodrend](Bloodrend.md) [ResistanceFlamecharm](Flamecharm.md) [ResistanceGalebreathe](Galebreathe.md) [ResistanceShadowcast](Shadowcast.md) [ResistanceThundercall](Thundercall.md).
