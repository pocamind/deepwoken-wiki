## Description

The Harvestman is a [Hallowtide](Hallowtide.md) [World Boss](Bosses.md#world-bosses) encountered in [The Harvest Ritual](The_Harvest_Ritual.md), accessible by his [World Event](World_Events.md). They are a towering scarecrow wearing a long, ragged black robe tied together by a red cloth, alongside a cloak with pumpkin shoulder pads and numerous layers of tattered cloth. The Harvestman's head is a pile of straw emanating an orange glow, while having a radiating yellow halo at floating above it. He also has a second pair of mechanical arms that are primarily used in combat.

Unlike all bosses, this boss involves a unique gimmick where the player is **only** allowed to fight using the [Pumpkin Pitch](Pumpkin_Pitch.md) Mantra. This boss is intended to be fought like a bullet hell, where the only way to avoid attacks is to move around them.

## Passives

-   **Cursed Burn** - The Harvestman's attacks inflict Cursed Burn on the player.
-   The Harvestman's Rules - The Harvestman cannot be attacked by anything else other than the [Pumpkin Pitch](Pumpkin_Pitch.md) mantra. Your light attack is changed to [Pumpkin Pitch](Pumpkin_Pitch.md) throw.
-   Joy for the Living - whenever a player becomes a [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md), all surviving players are awarded 2 [Joy](Joy.md).
-   The Unrelenting - Every player that gets turned into a [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md) during the boss now buffs surviving players, increasing their damage by 10% for every person.
-   Curse of the Gourdskipper - Dying to the Harvestman revives the player as a [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md).
    -   Attacking other players as a [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md) will infect them as well, and grants 10 [Joy](Joy.md) to the attacker.
    -   Using [Pumpkin Pitch](Pumpkin_Pitch.md) against an infected player will instantly defeat them, removing them from the [Dungeon](Dungeons.md).
    -   Killing a [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md) as a participant will restore 20% as a healthpack.
    -   Anytime another player in the server gets infected you gain 2 Joy.

## Tips and Strategy

The most important tip would be to prioritize dodging over dealing damage, as regenerating health in this bossfight is rather difficult, especially in solo runs.

It's best to try and dodge the Crowd Control attacks, as those are the ones likely to hit and deal lots of damage; as well as inflicting Cursed Flames, yet giving barely any opportunity to extinguish the flames by dodging.

Jumping in the bossfight isn't recommended as much, as for whatever reason, The Harvestman seems to pull you towards himself. It is currently unknown if it is a bug or not.

Whenever the The Harvestman is about to reach 50% of his health, stay nearby and try to be at the opposite direction whenever he is facing, as The Harvestman will begin his culling, launching a huge beam. This is best avoided by going staying close to The Harvestman, yet pay attention to the other attacks aside from the beam, as those can often prove lethal as well.

Utilizing the [Curse of the No Life King](Curse_of_the_No_Life_King.md) is recommended, it gives a free regeneration bonus for no downsides in the bossfight.

## Trivia

-   Many of the boss's attacks are based off of The Devourer of Gods from the _Terraria_ Calamity Mod.
-   The Harvestman's halo has words engraved into it: "at hallows' tide, with reaping blade o, ardenstar, we call thy name", a phrase that was shown in the Hallowtide 2026 trailer image.
