# Aelita Plush

## Description

The **Aelita Plush** was a plushie released as part of a Deepwoken and Makeship collaboration. Classified under the "Plushie" category, it resembled [Aelita](Aelita.md). Its campaign ran from September 1st, 2026, to September 23rd, 2026, before selling out. The plushie measured 21.2 cm in height, 11.7 cm in width, and 9.7 cm in depth. It was made from polypropylene cotton and plush fabric (ultra soft fleece).

### Official Description

The siblings Arlekino seek a new domain to host their games. Will you be the complacent accomplice willing to offer them such a space, rent-free? In return, your soul would remain your own, of course...
