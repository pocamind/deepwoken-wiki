# Account Tier

## Description

Account-Tiers are a hidden mechanic keeps track of a player's experience within Deepwoken, with currently 3 tiers being known to the Community.

It is not known when such system was introduced, with the most likely implementation having originated from the Bounty Reworks.

### Effects

In certain Game Aspects, you can only interact with your same Tier, such as obtaining [Voidwalker Origin](Voidwalker.md), or [Bounty-Hunting](Bounty_Hunting.md). (I.e: For Bounty-Hunting, the bounty will only appear to you, if you are within the same Tier.)

It is unknown currently if this also affects [Guild Scores](Guilds.md).

## Trivia

-   Once you go up a Tier, there is no way to go down. Additionally, It is impossible to skip Tiers.
-   You are only capable of going up Tiers if you gain the experience **yourself**. You are unable to just drag someone along for [Dungeons](Dungeons.md) until they reach Power 20 or other methods. They have to get Credits for Bosses, Enemies and Everything else.
