## Description

**Pomar** is a [Food](Food.md) item and crafting ingredient. It is also obtained from [Rogue Constructs](Rogue_Construct.md) and [Hive Constructs](Hive_Construct.md) as a direct drop.

It is a type of **Plant**, meaning it cannot be eaten with the [Carnivore](Talents.md#carnivore) Talent.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 14% Stomach and 9% Water.
