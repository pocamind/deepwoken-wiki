## Description

The Prototype Railblade is a [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md) exclusive [Greatsword](Weapons.md).

## Critical

For the **standard** critical, the user rushes forward and strikes the target with their shoulder, applying Knockdown. If this is landed, the attack will follow up with a 360° sweep. Only the first hit will apply knockdown. Cancellable in the initial windup, however becomes uncancellable as the attack itself unfolds.

For the **aerial** critical, the user leaps into the air, raising the blade overhead before unleashing a downwards slash, applying Knockdown.

The cooldown for either critical is **9 seconds**; they share a Critical cooldown.

Despite what is shown in the GIFs, the effects from the criticals are recolored white, reflecting on the design being a prototype.

These criticals have the M1 tag, allowing them to proc M1-exclusive [Talents](Talents.md) and [Enchantment](Enchantments.md) effects. It does **not** proc Flame or Flame-related talents.

## Obtainment

The Prototype Railblade can be found at the [Saramaed Summit](Saramaed_Summit.md).
