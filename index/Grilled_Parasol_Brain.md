## Description

**Grilled Parasol Brain** is a craftable [Food](Food.md) item. It is the result of cooking [Raw Parasol Brain](Raw_Parasol_Brain.md) at a [Campfire](Campfire_Pit.md) [CraftingCampfire](Campfire_Pit.md).

It is a type of **Meat**, meaning it cannot be eaten with the [Vegetarian](Boons_and_Flaws.md#vegetarian) Flaw.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 80% Stomach and 20% Water.  
  
Additionally, it grants the Brain buff, affecting 15% of the Stomach bar. The buff increases the effectiveness or efficiency of your [Deep Gems](Deep_Gems.md);

-   [Blessed Gem](Blessed_Gem.md): Mantra cooldown reduction is increased from -50% to -66%.
-   [Bloodless Gem](Bloodless_Gem.md): Bloodless' healing cap is increased by 5 (15 to 20).
-   [Might Gem](Might_Gem.md): Posture damage buff is increased by 5%, additively (+10% to +15%).
-   [Nocturnal Gem](Nocturnal_Gem.md): Damage is increased by 5 (10 to 15).
-   [Wayward Gem](Wayward_Gem.md): Effect cooldown is decreased by 5 seconds (8s to 3s).
-   [Wrath Gem](Wrath_Gem.md): Critical damage buff per-combat tag is doubled (7.5% per-combat tag to 15%).
-   [Aegis Gem](Aegis_Gem.md), [Wind Gem](Wind_Gem.md): Effect duration and Gem cooldown is increased by 5 seconds (10s to 15s).
-   [Blue Gem](Blue_Gem.md), [Insignia Gem](Insignia_Gem.md), [Warped Blue Gem](Warped_Blue_Gem.md): No noticeable difference.
