## Description

**Bephalos** was a member of [The Ministry](The_Ministry.md) who orchestrated the assassination of [Rolen Vashtir](Rolen_Vashtir.md), allowing [Zi'eer](The_Lord_Regent.md) to seize control of Etrea.[\[1\]](#cite-note-sb-1)

Bephalos murdered an Etrean Blade Initiate named Rali and manipulated her friend, Tavi Ytreshi, into believing the King was responsible for her death and those of many other Etreans.[\[1\]](#cite-note-sb-1) When Tavi was selected as one of four guards accompanying the King to [Markor](Markor.md) for diplomatic talks with [The Central Authority](The_Central_Authority.md), Bephalos gifted him the Queensblade, a Depths Relic capable of ensuring its victims remained in the Depths, and promised to meet him again on "the night of the last star."[\[1\]](#cite-note-sb-1)

The night before their departure, Bephalos performed the final ritual required for the blade to seal the King in the Depths. After Tavi successfully assassinated the King, Bephalos revealed that he had manipulated him, explaining that the other guards would kill him and that history would remember him as a murderer. He offered Tavi an escape from his burdens in exchange for sinking the ship and killing its crew. Tavi accepted, and Bephalos sent him away before sinking the vessel.[\[1\]](#cite-note-sb-1)

With the King's fate unknown, [Etrea](Etrea.md) believed he had been lost to the [Voidsea](Voidsea.md). Zi'eer subsequently assumed the title of Lord Regent, brainwashed the populace, and began preparing for another Great Drowning. The King's failure to arrive in Markor likely also contributed to the Central Authority's eastward expansion, sparking an ongoing war.

    -   This theory was implicitly debunked when the story was retconned as a fictional tale.
