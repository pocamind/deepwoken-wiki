## Description

Outpost Wrath is a Location in _Deepwoken_. It is an outpost where [Authority Ensigns](Authority_Ensign.md) can receive and turn-in missions stationed near [Summer Isle](Summer_Isle.md) in [The Vashtir Sea](The_Vashtir_Sea.md).

## Content

### Captain Rendez

[Captain Rendez](Captain_Rendez.md) can be found inside of the outpost to receive and turn-in missions.
