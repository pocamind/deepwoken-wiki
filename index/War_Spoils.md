# War Spoils

| War Spoils | |
|---|---|
| usage | Exchanging items with Raudra, The Winged Lion |
| obtainment | Raudra, The Winged Lion |

“ Trophies extracted from your victims, those who do battle for battle's sake may value such grisly takings. ”

― War Spoils' description

## Overview

War Spoils are a type of currency that is obtained from Raudra.

### **Obtaining**

War Spoils are obtained by compleding Raudra's daily missions.

### **Usage**

You can exchange War Spoils for items and equipment by talking to Raudra.

## **Trivia**

-   War Spoils are an account wide currency
-   You cannot do Raudra's missions twice on the same account as a result
