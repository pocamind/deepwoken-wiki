## Description

Outpost Dignity is a Location in _Deepwoken_. It is an outpost where [Authority Ensigns](Authority_Ensign.md) can receive and turn-in missions stationed at [The Starswept Valley](The_Starswept_Valley.md).

## Content

### Captain Massi

[Captain Massi](Captain_Massi.md) can be found inside of the outpost to receive and turn-in missions.
