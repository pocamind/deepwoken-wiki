# Ameda

## Description

Ameda is a celestial object worshipped by the [Kyrsans](Kyrsgarde.md). It is their unique depiction of the Suncross. Within Old Kyrsa is the [Amedan Temple](Amedan_Temple.md) which still stands amongst the ruins of the city, but none make their pilgrimage to to the temple.
