## Description

Mantra Collectors are all [Felinor](Felinor.md) NPCs that will purchase your [Mantra Modifiers](Mantra_Modifiers.md), and [Treasures](Treasures.md).

## Location

They can be found on the at:

-   [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md), Loaf can be found near [Quyen](Quyen.md). Bagel is found on the Docks of the island near [Tone](Tone.md).
-   [Summer Isle](Summer_Isle.md), Brioche will be inside the castle.
-   [Miner's Landing](<Miner's_Landing.md>), Bagel will be on a rug near the on a edge of the [Saramaed Summit](<Saramaed_Summit_(Vow_of_Iron).md>)
