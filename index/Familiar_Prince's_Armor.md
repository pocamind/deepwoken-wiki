**Familiar Prince's Armor** is a Unique [Outfit](Outfits.md) in _Deepwoken_.

## Statistics

### _Requirements_

This Outfit costs 5,000 and requires five (5) [Cloth](Cloth.md), five (5) [Fiber](Fiber.md), one (1) [Curse of the No Life King](Curse_of_the_No_Life_King.md) [Enchant Stone](Enchant_Stone.md), one (1) [Bloodless Gem](Bloodless_Gem.md), and two (2) [Darkblood Vial](Darkblood_Vial.md) to craft.

This Outfit additionally requires the player to be at least [Power](Power.md) 15 to be crafted.

### _Armor Stats_

This Outfit has 3250 durability and grants +35% Physical resistance ResistancePhysical, and +35% Elemental resistance [ResistanceElemental](Attunements.md). Additionally, it provides the [Light Piercer](Talents.md#light-piercer) Talent.

The [Light Piercer](Talents.md#light-piercer) Talent grants an active Talent tool that only can be activated while wielding a [Spear](Iron_Spear.md). On use, this shoots out 3 light spear projectiles that pierce through your opponent, and their block.

Upon landing, they deal 10 physical damage per hit (3) and pierce through the target, continuing along their trajectory until parried or they reach their maximum distance of 160 studs. These have 100% chip damage. This has a 0.3s windup with a 10 second cooldown.

Additionally, while using the **Familiar Prince's Armor**, all passive regeneration is increased by 10%, multiplicatively.

## Trivia

-   This outfit is based on Rogue's Lineage's [Deep Knight's](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Knight) armor, the Deep Knight Armor.
-   Similarly to [Familiar Heretic's Armor](<Familiar_Heretic's_Armor.md>) and [Familiar Knight's Armor](<Familiar_Knight's_Armor.md>), this armor has high resistances, as Deep Knight is a notoriously tanky class with access to a few healing abilities.
-   Despite this armor appearing visually to be the [Deep Knight's](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Knight) armour, even including the prince fact the regeneration bonus is the highest in that game, Light Piercer actually comes from the [Church Knight.](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Church_Knight)
-   The weapon ability does not cancel out [Whistling Periapt](Whistling_Periapt.md) while actively using it.
