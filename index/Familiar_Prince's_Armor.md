**Familiar Prince's Armor** is a Unique [Outfit](Outfits.md) in _Deepwoken_.

## Statistics

### _Requirements_

This Outfit costs 5,000 and requires five (5) [Cloth](Cloth.md), five (5) [Fiber](Fiber.md), one (1) [Curse of the No Life King](Curse_of_the_No_Life_King.md) [Enchant Stone](Enchant_Stone.md), one (1) [Bloodless Gem](Bloodless_Gem.md), and two (2) [Darkblood Vial](Darkblood_Vial.md) to craft.

This Outfit additionally requires the player to be at least [Power](Power.md) 15 to be crafted.

### _Armor Stats_

This Outfit has 3250 durability and grants +35% Physical resistance ResistancePhysical, and +35% Elemental resistance [ResistanceElemental](Attunements.md). Additionally, it provides the [Light Piercer](Talents.md#light-piercer) Talent.

The [Light Piercer](Talents.md#light-piercer) Talent grants an active Talent tool that only can be activated while wielding a spear, shoots out 3 light spears that pierce through your opponent and their block.

Does little to no hitstun, Can be parried or dodged, Block ignores.

Does 10 physical damage per light spear hit and has great range of 160-170 (around the same as ardour slicer), has around 0,3s windup and 8 second cooldown.

While using the **Familiar Prince's Armor**, all passive regeneration is increased by 10%.
