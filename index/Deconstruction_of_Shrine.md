## Description

A painting of a deconstructed [Deep Shrine](Deep_Shrines.md). This shield is exclusively sold by [Traan Zakshun](Traan_Zakshun.md) for 10 in his black market.

The **Max Posture Bonus** stat can be increased with **Weight** Quality Stars.

## Trivia

-   Deconstruction of Shrine offers the worst stats of any shield, even being outclassed by [Targe](Targe.md), which has a requirement of 10 fortitude compared to Deconstruction of Shrine's 15 while having double the Posture bonus.
