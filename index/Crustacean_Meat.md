## Description

**Crustacean Meat** is a [Food](Food.md) item and crafting ingredient. It is also obtained from [Crustaceous Rex](Crustaceous_Rex.md) as a direct drop exclusively.

It is a type of **Meat**, meaning it cannot be eaten with the [Vegetarian](Boons_and_Flaws.md#vegetarian) Flaw.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 2% Stomach and 2% Water.
