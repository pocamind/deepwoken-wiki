## Description

**The Mad Steward** was a scholar who, following the rising of the Tides after [the Canticlysm](The_Canticlysm.md), sought the aid of the [Elder Edenkite](Edenkite.md) to prevent the [Voidsea](Voidsea.md) from consuming the remaining land. After one refused to provide an answer, he killed it and devoured its heart in an attempt to gain its knowledge, instead transforming into the first [Dread Serpent](Dread_Serpent.md).[\[1\]](#cite-note-lf-1)

The Mad Steward travelled to the [Northern Luminant](Luminants.md#northern-luminant) on a pilgrimage to commune with one of the nine Elder Edenkites, seeking a way to halt the rising Tides.[\[1\]](#cite-note-lf-1) When the Edenkite explained that humanity's rise and fall was inevitable, he interpreted its words as confirmation that they were responsible for the world's destruction.[\[1\]](#cite-note-lf-1) He beheaded the creature and consumed its heart, hoping to acquire the knowledge necessary to save humanity. However, this flooded his mind with the knowledge of every tragedy that had ever occurred, leaving him consumed by dread and transforming him into a twisted imitation of an Edenkite. He subsequently fled into the Voidsea, where he remained until his death.[\[1\]](#cite-note-lf-1)

Over time, the Dread Serpents multiplied, rendering the Voidsea nearly impassable. The Mad Steward eventually attacked and sank the Petra's Dawn during its return voyage between Luminants, swallowing its [anchor](<Petra's_Anchor.md>).

Later, a group of sailors encountered and slew the first Dread Serpent, finally putting the Mad Steward to rest. His [Murmur](Murmur.md), [Ardour](Murmur%253A_Ardour.md), was inherited by his killer. Long after his death, Dread Serpents continued to terrorize Voidsea travelers, with their [teeth](<Dread_Serpent's_Tooth.md>) eventually being used to harness their [Dread Breath](Monster_Mantras.md#dread-breath) ability. Each Dread Serpent also inherited his Murmur, making them targets for adventurers seeking its power.
