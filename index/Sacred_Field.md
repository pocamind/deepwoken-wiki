# Sacred Field

| Sacred Field | |
|---|---|
| effect | Protects you and allies from harm substantially. |
| appearance | Various colored field that spreads around the user. |
| cooldown | 45 Seconds + Duration |

“ Conjure a field of sacred light, protecting you and your allies from harm. ”

― Sacred Field description

## Description

**Sacred Field** is a [Resonance](Resonance.md) or "bell" that players can obtain, and is uncommon to roll.

### _Appearance_

When used, an orange, red, blue, green or purple field will appear around the user. Depending on the Resonance Points, the field can last anywhere from 20 seconds to around 1 minute.

## Usage

Pressing C will activate the **Sacred Field** Resonance. Holding C to activate will make the **Sacred Field** affect non-allies too.

**Sacred Field** will spawn an area that grants you and your allies defense against physical and elemental damage. The area isn't a field, yet a sphere meaning you can be under and over the area and you will still get the effect of **Sacred Field.**

If Sacred Field is corrupted, the field will follow its user instead of staying where it was placed.

### _Resonance Point usage_

When obtained, Sacred Field will spend its points on the following stats:

-   **Resistance type:** Decides the type of resistance that will be used. These are mutually exclusive with one another, meaning you can only have one resistance type.
    1.  Physical Resistance reduces Physical ResistancePhysical damage by **?%**.
    2.  Elemental Resistance reduces Elemental [ResistanceElemental](Attunements.md) damage by **?%** and Physical ResistancePhysical damage by **?%**.
    3.  Sanity Resistance reduces [Sanity](Insanity.md) [IconSanity](Insanity.md) loss by **?%**.
-   **Range:** The more points are spent on this stat, the larger the area covered by the resonance is.
-   **Duration:** The more points are spent on this stat, the longer the resonance lasts.
-   **Color:** A free stat. Changes the color of the field, the color is always random, but always vibrant.

## Trivia

-   **Sacred Field's** usage can allow players to focus less on their health and more on damage output.
-   When in a fight, using this next to a healing station will allow you to out heal damage. This most to only applies to Physical resist fields though.
-   This resonance works with any build just like [Portal](Portals.md), [Blood Scourge](Blood_Scourge.md), and etc. It is a good support bell for you and your team party.
-   **Sacred Field** is very useful for fighting large groups of people or bosses such as Duke.
-   **Sacred Field** can't be used in the [Voidsea](Voidsea.md). If corrupt, the drawback still applies.
