## Description

Outpost Virtue is a Location in _Deepwoken_. It is an outpost where [Authority Ensigns](Authority_Ensign.md) can receive and turn-in missions stationed at [Songseeker Wilds](Songseeker_Wilds.md).

## Content

### Captain Klave

[Captain Klave](Captain_Klave.md) can be found inside of the outpost to receive and turn-in missions.
