## Description

Jungle Hashi are NPCs that can only be found outisde of the [Mineskipper](Mudskipper.md#mineskipper) Mineshaft in [Songseeker Wilds](Songseeker_Wilds.md). They appear to patrol the area where [Thula](Thula.md), [Ignacio](Ignacio.md) and [Amethyst](<Amethyst_(NPC).md>) are assigned to.

Jungle Hashi wears a [Summer Company Uniform](Summer_Company_Uniform.md) outfit with their [aspect](Aspects.md) varying, a [Summer Scarf](Summer_Scarf.md), [Leather Boots](Leather_Boots.md), and a tricorn adorned with the [The Summer Company](The_Summer_Company.md)'s emblem.
