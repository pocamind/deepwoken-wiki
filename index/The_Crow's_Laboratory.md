# The Crow's Laboratory

LocationL The Crow's Laboratory LocationR

This is surely not a place of honour, for no deed of virtue is commemorated here.

  

## Description

**The Crow's Laboratory** was a sub-location on the [Isle of the Crows](Isle_of_the_Crows.md). The purpose of that laboratory is unknown, however as mentioned by Agamatsu[\[1\]](#cite-note-1) it is linked to [The Ministry](The_Ministry.md). Agamatsu also stated that "it didn't have much inside anyways."

## Trivia

-   It is believed that this area was reintroduced as [Duke Erisia's Laboratory](<Duke_Erisia's_Laboratory.md>).
