## Description

The Thorned Blazer is an [Equipment](Equipment.md) item under the "Arms" category.

The [Cursed Thorns](Talents.md#cursed-thorns) Talent makes monsters who hit you take 20% of the damage they deal to you, but you now also take 10% more damage from monsters. Landing [Pumpkin Pitch](Pumpkin_Pitch.md) on a player makes them susceptible to this effect for a few seconds.
