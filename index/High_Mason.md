## Description

A High Mason also known as Head Deathscribe is the leader of the [Memorial Masons](Memorial_Masons.md) responsible for maintaining the [Hall of Memory](Hall_of_Memory.md) and preserving the names of [Divers](The_Divers.md) who have died or retired from service.[\[1\]](#cite-note-song-1)
