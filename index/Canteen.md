# Canteen

| Canteen | |
|---|---|
| usage | Holds liquids |
| recipe | x1 Calabash and x1 Beeswax at a Crafting Station |

## Description

The _Canteen_ is a utility item used to store and carry water. A Canteen can store up to 5 uses before needing to be replenished. Players can spawn with a Canteen by obtaining the "Advanced Tools: Canteen" [Echo Upgrade](Echoes.md).

### Uses

The Canteen can be filled by holding it out and pressing M1 near most liquid sources.

Freshwater sources include [Water Wells](Water_Well.md) and flowing sources of water such as rivers located around Starswept Valley, Merit's Reach, Greathive Aratel, Minityrsa, and Songseeker Wilds. Attempting to drink water collected from the ocean or acid, such as the pools found in [Burning Stone Gardens](Burning_Stone_Gardens.md), causes the player to vomit, resulting in a considerable loss of stomach and water.

## Trivia

-   The pool of ichor located under the [Celestial](Celestial.md) in the [Derelict Highchurch](Derelict_Highchurch.md) can be collected.
-   The pools of "honey" in [Greathive Aratel](Greathive_Aratel.md) can be collected.
-   The water from the fishing pond room in [Guild Bases](Guild_Bases.md) can be collected.
-   The [Old Stranger](Old_Stranger.md) rarely found at sea will request freshwater. Speaking to him while carrying a Canteen filled with freshwater will grant the player [Ardour](Murmur%253A_Ardour.md). Players without a [Murmur](Murmur.md) may therefore benefit from carrying a full Canteen of freshwater.
-   Filling up a canteen with ocean water and then leaving the game will remove its unsafe properties, allowing for an unconventional but potentially life-saving way to satiate your water.
