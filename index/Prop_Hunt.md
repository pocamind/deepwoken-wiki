# Prop Hunt

“ You are invited to an unorthodox game of hide-and-seek on the mysterious Isle of Crows... ”

― World Event timer

  

## Game Mode

Prop Hunt is a [Hallowtide](Hallowtide.md)\-exclusive gamemode, added in the [Hallowtide 2026](Version_History/2026.md#october-31st-2026) event. Similarly to [World Events](World_Events.md), a pop-up on the right hand side of the screen will be shown globally when the event is starting. Upon selecting to join, you will be transported to the Isle of the Crows after a short delay.

In this gamemode, all players are randomly selected to be either a Hider or a Seeker. The Hiders are given **one minute** to find a hiding spot in the [Corvus Manor](Corvus_Manor.md), before a Seeker is chosen from one of the Hiders and the minigame begins.

### Hiders

The Hiders gain the ability to disguise themselves as a prop (e.g. a Barrel or Pumpkin) by using the Prop Selector tool, to attempt to blend-in with the Manor's environment. Periodically a high-pitched whistle sound effect will play from the Hiders, potentially notifying the Seekers that there are Hiders nearby. This is triggered at a different interval for each Hider; hiding near other Hiders may get you caught.

The Hiders are tasked with waiting out the time limit without getting caught by the Seekers. If a Seeker catches a Hider, then that Hider will **become** a Seeker, with their goal changing to finding the remaining Hiders.

Note: Console players cannot shift between the prop selector pages, as the Q and E inputs were not routed to any console button.

### Seekers

It is the goal of all Seekers to find the Hiders; they only win if **all** Hiders are caught.

Seekers can catch Hiders by attacking props with basic attacks. If a Seeker uses a basic attack and **doesn't** hit a Hider, they will lose 20% of their maximum health as a penalty. If they **do** hit a Hider however, they will catch them, healing to maximum health ane turning the caught player into a Seeker.

## Rewards

-   All Seekers will be awarded with 10 [Joy](Joy.md), with an additional 10 Joy per Hider they caught.
-   All remaining Hiders (if any) will be awarded with 3 [Goodie Bags](Goodie_Bag.md) and 60 Joy.

    -   Seekers will wear: [Sanguine Finery](Sanguine_Finery.md).

-   The whistle sound effect that is played from Hiders was taken from the [Beast Whistle](Mounts.md) tool.
