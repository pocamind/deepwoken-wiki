## Description

Kiternon was a [Felinor](Felinor.md) NPC. They asked the player to bring someone of greater strength than your own power.

## Location

Kiternon was located inside of the house containing a [Cauldron](Cauldron.md) at [Fort Merit](Fort_Merit.md).
