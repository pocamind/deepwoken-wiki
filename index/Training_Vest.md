## Description

The **Training Vest** is a [Training Gear](Training_Gear.md) used to increase the player's [Fortitude](Attributes.md#fortitude).

In order to train your Fortitude, you'll have to put on the vest. Fortitude will increase upon moving around, taking posture damage, dodging attacks, and taking damage all with the vest on.

The vest provides an innate +5% Physical Armor while equipped.

## Usage

To use the Training Vest, hold it out and then M1 again to put it on. The vest will only lose durability after unequipping only Fortitude was trained while it was equipped.
