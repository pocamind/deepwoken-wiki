The **Fine Ink**, **Paperette**, and **Shell Stock** are items used for the _Deepwoken_ [Hallowtide](Hallowtide.md) event to craft the [Hallowtide Mask](Hallowtide_Mask.md).

These items are found within chests of any mob during the event The items have no other use besides crafting the Hallowtide Mask. The items can be dropped and will stay in the inventory even after Hallowtide ends.

## Trivia

-   The Fine Ink jars use the same model as the jars of blood used by [Chaser](Chaser,_Scholar_of_the_Crimson_Contract.md).
-   On release, Void feathers cost 5 Joy instead of the displayed 15. This was patched as of [November 5th, 2022](Version_History/2022-2021.md#november-5th-2022).
