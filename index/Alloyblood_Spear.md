## Description

The Alloyblood Spear is one of three Alloyblood Weapons and is a [Spear](Iron_Spear.md) in the Medium Weapons category. Conjured from the Alloyblood itself, this weapon boasts innate [Bleed](Status_Effects.md#bleed) and increased range, with the range being further improved with its Talent's effect ([see below](#talents)).

This weapon can only be obtained by using the [Spinning Serum](Hybrid_Abilities.md#spinning-serum) Alloyblood Mantra after building up enough [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) on the target via [Bloodrend](Bloodrend.md) and [Ironsing](Ironsing.md) attacks. This weapon has a set duration after the Mantra is used.

## Alloyblood Upgrades

This weapon can be further enhanced through the [Composite Path: Alloyblood](Talents.md#composite-path-alloyblood) Talents:

-   [Stored Plasma](Talents.md#stored-plasma) and [Retained Hemoglobin](Talents.md#retained-hemoglobin) increase the duration of the Alloyblood Spear, after the player feints or casts a different Alloyblood Weapon Mantra, respectively.
-   [Ultrafiltration](Talents.md#ultrafiltration) allows the weapon attacks on Alloyblood Spear to apply the [Metal Rods](Status_Effects.md#metal-rods) and [Blood Poison](Status_Effects.md#blood-poison) status effects. These status effects are normally replaced with [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) with this Attunement Path, so this is very impactful.
-   [Plasma Refresh](Talents.md#plasma-refresh) refreshes your critical attack cooldown upon using an Alloyblood Mantra, allowing you to use your weapon critical again immediately after swapping to the Alloyblood weapon. Additionally, it allows the Alloyblood weapons to apply [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) buildup on critical attacks.

## Talents

Equipping the Alloyblood Spear grants its unique [weapon Talent](Talents.md#weapon-talents)(s). Weapon Talents will be lost upon equipping any other weapon, including ability-bound weapons, like from the [Crazy Slots](Crazy_Slots.md) Resonance.

[**Iron Leech**](Talents.md#iron-leech) improves the range and swing speed of the spear based on how much armor durability and [Blood Poison](Status_Effects.md#blood-poison) your opponent has. The more Blood Poison, the more swing speed, and the lower the armor durability, the more range you get.

This synergizes with the [Alloyblood Daggers](Alloyblood_Daggers.md), as they apply Blood Poison and drain armor durability with their alternating light attacks, via their [Quick Incisions](Talents.md#quick-incisions) Talent. Alternatively, the [Ultrafiltration](Talents.md#ultrafiltration) Talent will allow all Alloyblood Weapons to apply Blood Poisoning (and Metal Rods) on their basic attacks, and the player may use the [Metal](Metal.md) Enchantment or other sources of armor drain as well.

## Critical

Alloyblood Spear uses the default Spear critical, a horizontal swing followed by a forward thrust. It has a scaled damage multiplier of **1x** and a cooldown of **5 seconds**. Both hits deal **5** posture damage.

This critical will be canceled if the first hit is parried.  
  
This critical has the M1 tag, allowing it to proc M1-exclusive [Talents](Talents.md) and [Enchantment](Enchantments.md) effects.
