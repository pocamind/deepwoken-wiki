## Description

The Sanguine Candle is an item obtainable from [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md) chests. It's used to spawn [Jungle Jim](Jungle_Jim.md), [Phantom Hunter](Phantom_Hunter.md), and [Bloodstarved Hunter](Bloodstarved_Hunter.md) with specific weapons tied to them, Specific ones can be spawned via interacting with Strange Sigils around the entire map, or alternatively just be placed down and lit to spawn a random one. The NPCS drop the weapon they are wielding upon killing them.
