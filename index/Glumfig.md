## Description

**Glumfig** is a [Food](Food.md) item. It is also purchased from [Traan Zakshun](Traan_Zakshun.md) for 2,500 or 5.

It is a type of **Plant**, meaning it cannot be eaten with the [Carnivore](Talents.md#carnivore) Talent.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 20% Stomach, but depletes -20% Water.

## Trivia

-   Glumfigs were first mentioned ingame from an NPC dialogue exchange. Nearly a month after this dialogue exchange was added, the Glumfig was officially added to the game.
-   Upon release, the Glumfig had the most potent instant health effect for potion effects, healing ~20 health per fig. This was so potent in fact that it created its own potion type, the _Rejuvenating **Remedy**_. This of course was a bug and was patched shortly afterwards.
-   Glumfigs have been heavily affected by Deepwoken's rampant inflation problem, as their current cost is well over double what they were originally sold for, being 1,000 or 1.
