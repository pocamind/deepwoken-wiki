## Description

Linh is an [Etrean](Etrean.md) NPC.

Their counterpart in the [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md) is [Quyen](Quyen.md).

## Location

Linh is located in [Etris](<Etris_(Vow_of_Iron).md>), in front of the abandoned [Antiquarian](Antiquarian.md) building. They are standing outside of a house.
