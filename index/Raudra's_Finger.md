## Description

**Raudra's Finger** is a Ring [Equipment](Equipment.md) that gains more damage by being combat-tagged by mobs and players.

The [Raudra's Pride](Talents.md#raudras-pride) Talent you gain +3% damage for each player and +1% damage for each mob that has combat-tagged you. At 5 stacks, Vent to burn your stacks and reset all cooldowns.

At 10 Raudra's Fingers, this provides a 4.5% damage buff per combat tag you have, as long as you have at least four combat tags.

With each [Battle Royale](Battle_Royale.md) win the player will cosmetically gain more Raudra's Fingers on their hands. The maximum number of fingers is 10.

## Trivia

-   Despite what the description of item may lead you to believe, it actually activates at 4 combat tags and not 3.
-   Originally known as "Shiva's Finger".
