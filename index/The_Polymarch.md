# The Polymarch

**[Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md) Exclusive**

This content is not accessible outside of this gamemode.

## Overview

**The Polymarch** is a massive flagship belonging to [the Hundred Legions](The_Hundred_Legions.md). It houses 4 Polymarch Legion Soldiers and the memory task location of General Titus Juventius.

## Points of Interest

-   [General Titus Juventius](General_Titus_Juventius.md)
-   Legion Ship [Boundary](<Boundary_(Vow_of_Iron).md>)

## Contents

This ship is the area where General Titus Juventius and his Legion resides. Titus can be spawned on the deck in the middle of the legion shield, and other notable parts of the ship include the bowsprit which Titus may stand on if a player he was previously fighting is defeated, and also the Cargo Hold which is below the deck. The Cargo Hold is a safe option for fighting Titus in case you get hit by his Throw move. Inside the Cargo Hold is a ladder which leads to the Legion Ship boundary as well as the Cabin which connects the Cargo Hold to the Deck. However, due to Boundary being located at the top of the ladder, the ladder is effectively rendered useless as attempting to use it will just send you through the Boundary.

## Trivia

-   If you plan on fighting General Titus Juventius, consider placing a boat on either side of the ship, as if you were to fall off of it, there is no easy way to get back on, leaving you vulnerable to most of Titus' attacks.
-   In order to avoid reentering the Legion Ship [Boundary](<Boundary_(Vow_of_Iron).md>), you can jump off of the wall to the left of the ladder and then stand on a ledge opposite the boundary. Ensure you are not too close to the boundary otherwise you will just jump right back in.
