**Familiar Sage's Armor** is a Unique [Outfit](Outfits.md) in _Deepwoken_.

## Statistics

### _Requirements_

This Outfit costs 5,000 and requires five (5) [Cloth](Cloth.md), five (5) [Fiber](Fiber.md), one (1) [Ferryman's Coat Cloth](<Ferryman's_Coat_Cloth.md>), one (1) [Storm](Storm.md) [Enchant Stone](Enchant_Stone.md), and one (1) [Stormbreaker](Stormbreaker.md) [Enchant Stone](Enchant_Stone.md) to craft.

This Outfit additionally requires the player to be at least [Power](Power.md) 15 to be crafted.

### _Armor Stats_

This Outfit has 2750 durability and grants +25% Physical resistance ResistancePhysical, +25% Elemental resistance [ResistanceElemental](Attunements.md), +10% Lightning Armor [ResistanceThundercall](Thundercall.md), and +50% Ether Regen [IconEther](Ether.md). Additionally, it provides the [Lightning Drop](Talents.md#lightning-drop) Talent.

The [Lightning Drop](Talents.md#lightning-drop) Talent grants a Talent tool that upon activation and being airborne (studs distance unknown), you quickly drop down and strike opponents near where you land with lightning.

## Trivia/Notes

-   This armor is a reference to the 'Dragon Sage' class from monad studios other game rogue lineage.
