## Description

The Corvidirge Severants is a pair of two purple scythes adorned with feathers and the claws of a Harrow, a creature hailing from [The Boundary](Lore.md#the-boundary). This weapon is categorized as both a [Twinblades](Twinblades.md) and a [Dagger](Daggers.md), making it both compatible for [Talents](Talents.md) in their respective weapon categories.

## Critical

The user spins around with both blades, [dazing](Status_Effects.md#daze) the opponent every hit. It is followed by the user transforming into two crows that slowly converge together, releasing a blast that dazes those within its radius.

The critical has a cooldown of **10 seconds**.

## Trivia

-   This weapon is treated as a **Light Weapon** for most [Enchantments](Enchantments.md).
-   The Medium Silentheart abilities will be used when the Light ones are on cooldown.
