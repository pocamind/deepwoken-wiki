# Ardenstar family

| Ardenstar family | |
|---|---|

The Ardenstar family, a noble family of Lightborn that once resided in Constellia, or "The Guiding Star". They were vassals to the Spellhardt family, both under the authority of the King of Constellia. Since the time their forebearers gifted them Constellia up until its Doom they coexisted in peace and fealty.

This was until the Kyrsan invasion. The Drowned God Ethiron waged a war on Constellia, and in the midst of the war the Celestials simply vanished, their whereabouts unknown, the morale of the Lightborn crushed as their "gods" as they once perceived them fled.

The Ardenstar however acknowledged their emminent loss and in an act of treachery conspired with the Kyrsgarde, they betrayed their King and helped breach the Constellian strongholds. Despite their efforts at diplomacy with the Kyrsgarde at the expense of their own people they were betrayed anyways by the Kyrsgarde.

Since the Spellhardt family remained loyal, for their vassal to betray their oaths of fealty lead to their hatred and detest for the Ardenstar, hence their beheading and mummifying of the Head Ardenstar.
