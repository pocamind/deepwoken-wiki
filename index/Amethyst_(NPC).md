## Description

Amethyst is an [Adret](Adret.md) NPC. She is a dragoon of [The Summer Company](The_Summer_Company.md).

## Location

Amethyst is located in a small opening of the mineshaft going up the [Songseeker Wilds](Songseeker_Wilds.md).
