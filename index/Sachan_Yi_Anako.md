## Description

Sachan Yi Anako is the leader of [Clan Anako](Clan_Anako.md) and one of the four sachans of the [Etrean Royalists](Etrean_Royalists.md). Yi herself is Irritable and brutally honest, traits which can make negotiations quite hard. Despite her traits she is still a warrior resolute on defeating the [Lord Regent](The_Lord_Regent.md).[\[3\]](#cite-note-junsui-3)[\[4\]](#cite-note-high-monk-junsui-4)

She sided with [Sachan Choji Mofisu](Sachan_Choji_Mofisu.md) in his desire to reclaim the [Temple of Mur](Temple_of_Mur.md) and the [Viridian Sanctuary](Viridian_Sanctuary_Ruins.md).[\[5\]](#cite-note-gold-5)

## Trivia

-   When the camera is clipped inside her katana, the blade is a red color reflecting her clan's color scheme.
-   She is the only one of her clan to have both Sabatons, the rest of her clan has only one Sabaton on their right foot.
