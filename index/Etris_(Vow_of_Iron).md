TERRITORY OF Etrea

High amongst the peaks of the mountains, the once-untouchable city now labours under siege.

  

## Overview

Etris is a much more dastardly location in [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md), with it being under constant warfare by a both [The Central Authority](The_Central_Authority.md) and the [Etrean Royalists](Etrean_Royalists.md). [Zi'eer, the Fourth Prophet](<Zi'eer,_the_Fourth_Prophet.md>) in his attempt to maintain his defense has set a numerous amount of his [Shogun's](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>) to protect the path to Etris.

## Points of Interest

### Buongustino's Abandoned Kitchen

-   [Chef Buongustino](Chef_Buongustino.md)'s abandoned kitchen.

### Guildhall

-   Players can still start a Guild by talking to the [Guild Clerk](Guild_Clerk.md) when they reach [Power](Power.md) 5.

  

### A For Sale building

-   An empty For Sale Building.

### The Barrier

-   To truly prevent anyone from halting his plans [Zi'eer](The_Lord_Regent.md) has set up an Emerald Barrier that covers the entirely of the [Etris Palace](Etris_Palace.md).

### The Den

-   The [Den Master](<Den_Master_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) can be found here.
-   To get there sail to the right of the docks and loop around the mountains.

### Alchemist's Lodge

-   The Etrea Home [Boundary](<Boundary_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) can be found here, and close by a young [Kennith](<Kennith_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) is playing with his toys.
