# Hall of Memory

The hall of memory is a Mortuary Wing within [The Citadel](The_Citadel_of_Markor.md). It is known as an area of Memorials and documents for all [Divers](The_Divers.md) who either

1.  Leave the organisation
2.  retirement

or Much more commonly,

3\. Death.

[Black divers](Black_Divers_division.md) get Gilded memorials to show their importance to [The Divers](The_Divers.md)
