## Description

An audacious Authority Warden was said to have slain a [Crimson Terrapod](Terrapod.md) on a newly emerged [Erisia](Erisia.md), using its [shell](Crimson_Terrapod_Shell.md) to craft an armor resembling the now known [Crimson Terraplate Pauldrons](Crimson_Terraplate_Pauldrons.md).[\[1\]](#cite-note-ct-1)

The Authority Warden was rumored to take on the aggressive mannerisms akin to Terrapods after wearing the pauldrons for too long.[\[1\]](#cite-note-ct-1)
