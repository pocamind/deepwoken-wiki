# Gliders

| Gliders | |
|---|---|
| usage | Allows the user to glide across long distances. |
| recipe | x2 Stick, x2 Cloth, x2 Stick, x2 Megalodaunt Hide |

## Description

Gliders are a utility that gives the player the ability to glide, allowing easy travel through terrain.

## Obtaining

Anyone is able to craft a Normal Glider at any point. Simply head to a [Crafting Station](Crafting_Station.md), use the Normal Glider recipe (x2 Stick, x2 Cloth) and craft it. For a Sharkscale Glider, head to a [Crafting Station](Crafting_Station.md) and use the Sharkscale Glider recipe instead (x2 Stick, x2 Megalodaunt Hide).

Both Gliders and Sharkscale Gliders can also be sold in [Merchant Ships](Travelling_Merchant.md).

Flimsy Gliders are obtained temporarily in modes such as [Battle Royale](Battle_Royale.md).

Feathered Gliders are granted to [Tirans](Tiran.md) on spawn via the [Feathered Glider](Talents/Categories_A-F.md) talent.

## Usage

The Glider can only be used in the air and cannot be used in Acid Rain (if you do not have the Survivalist Boon). It cannot be used in combat under any circumstances.

There is no difference between any of the Gliders except for their amount of uses. The Glider has 15 uses while the Sharkscale Glider has 150 uses. The Flimsy Glider has only 10 which drops down to 1 use when the user touches the ground. The Feathered Glider has infinite uses.

An addition to gliders, [Galebreathe](Galebreathe.md) users can eventually obtain "Aerogliding" Talent, which greatly decreases the rate you descend and greatly increases the distance you can travel with a glider, which is very useful to have, also obtain the ability to glide with galebreathe by holding space after falling, which is a substitute for a glider, but it goes down much faster, has little to no horizontal distance and expires extremely quickly, but it can be used in danger and is useful for escaping a fight by gliding away.

**BE CAREFUL.** Taking damage with a glider will Ragdoll your character which means flying in places that have acid rain such as [Upper Erisia](Upper_Erisia.md) could be dangerous. If you are suffering from ping issues, your glider may also delay heavily when you are trying to deploy it which is likely to cause fall damage from deploying the glider and subsequently causing you to ragdoll and plummet.

## Trivia

-   The Feathered Glider comes in 4 different colors, each matching the aspect variant of the Tiran using it.
-   Prior to the 21 June 2024 update, Gliders were required to be unlocked via a quest. Prior to a patch update on 9 May 2023, Gliders required a schematic to be craftable.
