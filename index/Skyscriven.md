# Skyscriven

## Description

Skyscriven is the religion of the [City of Celtor](City_of_Celtor.md), rooted in maritime culture and the worship of a pantheon of gods associated with the stars of the night sky. Officers were anointed at the [Cathedral of the Empyrean](Cathedral_of_the_Empyrean.md) and wore armor adorned with three stars.[\[1\]](#cite-note-1)
