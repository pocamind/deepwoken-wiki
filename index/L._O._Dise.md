## Description

Dise is a painter who painted [Deconstruction of Shrine](Deconstruction_of_Shrine.md).[\[1\]](#cite-note-lod-1)

## Trivia

-   The name “L.O. Dise” is a reference to LOD (Level of Detail), a gaming technique that uses less detailed models for objects that are farther away or when performance needs to be optimized.
