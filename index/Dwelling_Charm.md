## Description

Dwelling Charms allowed players to summon their [Guild Base](Guild_Bases.md) up to ten times until it ran out. It's main purpose was to allow players below the guild Officer rank to have an equivalent to the [Chime of Dwelling](Chime_of_Dwelling.md).

Dwelling Charms were able to be purchased at Guild Halls for 50 or inside of a Guild Base for 80.

## Trivia

-   The physical model was a white tag with red, with unreadable lettering on it.
-   Allowed [Voidwalkers](Voidwalker.md) that were actively on the hunt to place their Guild Base, even while under the effects of the Grasp of Eylis unlike the Chime of Dwelling.
