## Description

Winona Munet was a [Wakeguard](Wakeguard.md) of [the Central Authority](The_Central_Authority.md).[\[1\]](#cite-note-mj-1) In [Munet Journal](Munet_Journal.md), Winona described their grim duty of collecting fallen soldiers and watching over their bodies in an isolated stockyard, spending their days surrounded by corpses, flies, and maggots while waiting for someone to awaken and return to life.[\[1\]](#cite-note-mj-1) Bodies were kept in the stockyard for two weeks, allowing time for their souls to return from [the Depths](The_Depths.md) and restore their vessels.[\[1\]](#cite-note-mj-1)

Despite the disturbing nature of their work, Winona found genuine joy whenever a fallen soldier returns, welcoming them back to the surface and helping them through their confusion.[\[1\]](#cite-note-mj-1) However, most bodies never returned, leaving them alone with only their thoughts about mortality.[\[1\]](#cite-note-mj-1) They ultimately reflected on their own eventual death, imagining them self of becoming another vacant body lying in the stockyard.[\[1\]](#cite-note-mj-1)
