## Description

Tillian is a [Canor](Canor.md) NPC that is found exclusively during the [Carnival of Hearts](Carnival_of_Hearts.md) [World Event](World_Events.md) or in the Mocking Favor Talent from the [Jester's Garb](<Jester's_Garb.md>) outfit, giving a random boon or affliction when taunting.

He is a young, overly confident and charismatic albino Canor sporting long, messy, and spiky white hair. Tillian's pupils are unique in the way they resemble an upward purple crescent. Furthermore, he has orange eyeliner, comprised of a a 4-pointed star on one eye and a basic gradient on the other, surrounded by a grey eyeshadow. Tillian also has a piercing sitting below his lips. He dons the [Trickster's Habit](<Trickster's_Habit.md>) outfit and his own [beret](<Tillian's_Beret.md>).

He is the twin brother of [Aelita](Aelita.md). The two go by the name of the Siblings Arlekino. Like his sister, he is a wielder of Illusion Magic.
