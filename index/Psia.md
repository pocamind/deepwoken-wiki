## Description

Psia was a [Khan](Khan.md) NPC. They were believed to be able to increase the player's [Willpower](Attributes.md#wll) before [Training Gear](Training_Gear.md) became a commodity.

## Location

Psia was located sitting along the cliff side of [Etris](Etris.md) near [Lance](Lance.md)'s food.
