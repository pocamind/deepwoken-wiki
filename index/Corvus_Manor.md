# Corvus Manor

LocationL Corvus Manor LocationR

The infamous manor of House Corvus, a prominent noble family of Celtor. Like a heaving ship, it now cuts across the Voidsea, without a captain at the stern...

  

## Overview

The Corvus Manor is a large Celtorian Manor belonging to the Corvus family, it floats aimlessly though the [Voidsea](Voidsea.md) on the Isle of Crows.

With Hallowtide's arrival, [The Ferryman](The_Ferryman.md) makes himself at home and wishes to participate by playing a game of [Prop Hunt](Prop_Hunt.md) within the abandoned Manor.

## Contents

### Dining Hall

The Dining Hall is a large, open room within Corvus Manor, serving as a gathering place for its former inhabitants.

### Library

The library is a long hallway lined with shelves of books.

### The Study

The Study is tall room lined with bookshelves and paintings.

### Mausoleum

The Mausoleum is near the Corvus Manor, containing several coffins.

### Graveyard

The Graveyard is a burial ground containing several graves throughout the area.

### Isle of Crows

Due to a Strange fog that rolled in from the [Voidsea](Voidsea.md), most of the Isle of crows is inaccessible to the normal player. Dotted with the remnants of old structures and abandoned pathways.

### Paintings

Paintings can be found adorning the walls.
