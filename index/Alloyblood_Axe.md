## Description

The Alloyblood Axe is one of three Alloyblood Weapons and is a [Greataxe](Greataxes.md) in the Heavy Weapons category. Conjured from the Alloyblood itself, this weapon boasts higher crushing power, with this being further improved through its Talent's effect ([see below](#talents)).

This weapon can only be obtained by using the [Rising Plasma](Hybrid_Abilities.md#rising-plasma) Alloyblood Mantra after building up enough [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) on the target via [Bloodrend](Bloodrend.md) and [Ironsing](Ironsing.md) attacks. This weapon has a set duration after the Mantra is used.

## Alloyblood Upgrades

This weapon can be further enhanced through the [Composite Path: Alloyblood](Talents.md#composite-path-alloyblood) Talents:

-   [Stored Plasma](Talents.md#stored-plasma) and [Retained Hemoglobin](Talents.md#retained-hemoglobin) increase the duration of the Alloyblood Axe, after the player feints or casts a different Alloyblood Weapon Mantra, respectively.
-   [Ultrafiltration](Talents.md#ultrafiltration) allows the weapon attacks on Alloyblood Axe to apply the [Metal Rods](Status_Effects.md#metal-rods) and [Blood Poison](Status_Effects.md#blood-poison) status effects. These status effects are normally replaced with [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) with this Attunement Path, so this is very impactful.
-   [Plasma Refresh](Talents.md#plasma-refresh) refreshes your critical attack cooldown upon using an Alloyblood Mantra, allowing you to use your weapon critical again immediately after swapping to the Alloyblood weapon. Additionally, it allows the Alloyblood weapons to apply [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) buildup on critical attacks.
-   [Plasma Signal](Talents.md#plasma-signal) highlights your opponent once they reach enough [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) for you to be able to use Rising Plasma. When this happens, they also gain 10% more Composite Alloy buildup.
-   [Hypoferritinemia](Talents.md#hypoferritinemia) **replaces** the Alloyblood Greataxe with the [Metal Greatsword](Metal_Greatsword.md), summoning that weapon instead after Rising Plasma is cast. This will also give the Metal Greatsword 75% base Chip Damage, with this value further scaling based on how much [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) the opponent has.

## Talents

Equipping the Alloyblood Axe grants its unique [weapon Talent](Talents.md#weapon-talents)(s). Weapon Talents will be lost upon equipping any other weapon, including ability-bound weapons, like from the [Crazy Slots](Crazy_Slots.md) Resonance.

[**Composite Crusher**](Talents.md#composite-crusher) further increases the Alloyblood Axe's damage, scaling on how much [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) your opponent has.

## Critical

Alloyblood Axe uses the default Greataxe critical, an overhead swing. It has a scaled damage multiplier of **1x** and a cooldown of **3 seconds**. Grants **[Hyperarmor](Status_Effects.md#hyperarmor)** for the first 1 second of the attack's windup, ending just before your weapon comes down. On hit, it applies **[Knockdown](Status_Effects.md#knockdown)** and will insta-guardbreak opponents who attempt to block it.
