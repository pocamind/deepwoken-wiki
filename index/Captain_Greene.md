## Description

Captain Greene is a legendary figure known throughout the [Summer Company](The_Summer_Company.md) who grew stronger with wealth.[\[1\]](#cite-note-1)

He created the [Tenacity](Tenacity.md), an extremely durable shield he speculated that [Rosen](Gunsmith_Rosen.md)'s guns could not pierce. He was unable to test the shield against Rosen's arsenal, thus tossing the shield away.[\[2\]](#cite-note-2)
