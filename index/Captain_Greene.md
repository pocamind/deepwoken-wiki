## Description

Captain Greene was a legendary figure known throughout the [Summer Company](The_Summer_Company.md) who grew stronger with wealth.[\[1\]](#cite-note-1)

He created the [Tenacity](Tenacity.md) a shield hewn from the Doom Of Caeranthil's own scales under commission from the the [Summer Company](The_Summer_Company.md).
