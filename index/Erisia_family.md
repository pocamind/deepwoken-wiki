## Description

The Erisia family are a noble house of [Lost Celtor](Lost_Celtor.md) that reside over the island of [Erisia](Erisia.md). The current head of the family is [Duke Ishamon Erisia](Duke_Ishamon_Erisia.md), who became the head some time prior to 904 CE, and has remained the head during the 300 years that followed the drowning of Celtor.
