TERRITORY OF Etrean Royalists

The ancestral seat of the noble Clan Anako, a proud lineage of Etrean warriors, towers over the gloom that surrounds it.

  

## Overview

The Red Lotus Keep is an Etrean Royalists stronghold located near the [Derelict Highchurch](Derelict_Highchurch.md) at [Songseeker Wilds](Songseeker_Wilds.md).

## Points of Interest

You can purchase an additional [Etris Flask](Etris_Flask.md) at this location for 250. The Scarlet Citadel [Boundary](Boundary.md) is also located here near the exterior wall of a building.
