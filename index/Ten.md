## Description

Ten is the central figure behind the island [Simforea](Simforea.md) and the disappearance of the [Anansi](Anansi.md) race. They were an exceptionally ambitious [Anansi](Anansi.md) and the only known member of their race to awaken ten eyes, far beyond the six eyes Anansi traditionally sought through meditation.[\[1\]](#cite-note-1) They created an organ and cradle designed to draw souls from the [Moonseye](Moonseye.md) and inject them into their body.[\[2\]](#cite-note-manuscript-2)

Ten's experiment ultimately destroyed them. Their body could not withstand the souls they absorbed, causing them to mutate and develop increasingly more eyes until they lost control of their body.[\[2\]](#cite-note-manuscript-2) Their experiments also caused Simforea to deteriorate. Ten later lured the other Anansi to Simforea, and Ten's supernatural connection to them allowed them to sense the success of his cradle and follow him there. This ultimately led to the disappearance and death of the Anansi race.[\[2\]](#cite-note-manuscript-2)

Along all of this, Ten encountered [Knell](Knell,_The_Lunatic_Resonator.md) and promised him that he would finally obtain a [Resonance](Resonance.md).[\[3\]](#cite-note-3)
