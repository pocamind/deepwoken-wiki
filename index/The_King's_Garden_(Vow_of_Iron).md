## Overview

The King's Garden is a landmark in [Etris](<Etris_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) filled with [Shogun's](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>) as the last line of defense against [The Inheritor](The_Inheritor.md).

After defeating the [True Heart of Enmity](True_Heart_of_Enmity.md) the [Voices of the Depths](Voices_of_the_Depths.md) send you directly inside the emerald barrier. You must break the Statue to destroy the barrier. To re-enter later if you leave to prepare for [Zi'eer, the Fourth Prophet](<Zi'eer,_the_Fourth_Prophet.md>) you have to kill the [Shogun](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>) outside on the bridge.
