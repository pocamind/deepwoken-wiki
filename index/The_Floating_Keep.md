# The Floating Keep

LocationL The Floating Keep LocationR

The awe-inspiring moving keep of the mysterious [Spellhardt family](Spellhardt_family.md).

  

## Description

The Floating Keep is a temporary location in Deepwoken. (only found during Hallowtide) It appears to be a medieval keep, and very grand in appearance. The entire island is floating in the air next to the the Isle of Vigils. You can reach this place by interacting with the Jack'o Lantern in the [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md) near the building that sells attunement training items or at [Greathive Aratel](Greathive_Aratel.md) near the entrance. It is inhabited by [Lightborns](Lightborn.md) of the Spellhardt family. Here players can interact with members of the [Spellhardt family](Spellhardt_family.md) and participate in the Halloween Event.

### Cathedral

In the Cathedral, you can talk to [Lysander Spellhardt](Lysander_Spellhardt.md) to start the quest to craft yourself [a mask](Hallowtide_Mask.md). To create [the mask](Hallowtide_Mask.md), you need a [Shell Stock](Hallowtide_Mask_Ingredients.md), a [Paperette](Hallowtide_Mask_Ingredients.md) and a [Fine Ink](Hallowtide_Mask_Ingredients.md). When creating [the mask](Hallowtide_Mask.md), there is a chance for it to be of a Mudskipper, Primadon, or an Owl. Once you put on a mask, you can speak to NPCs and scare them (there are at least 87 NPCs you can scare, 13 in the depths and 74 in the overworld), which gives you Hallowtide Joy, which is a form of currency that can be exchanged with [Lysander](Lysander_Spellhardt.md), [Glaive](Glaive_Spellhardt.md), [Galpin](Galpin_Spellhardt,_Musicologist_Extraordinaire.md), and [Carmilla Spellhardt](Carmilla_Spellhardt.md) for items and unlocks. ### Events

-   Trial of the [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md)
    -   The trial consists of 8 waves that give 3 [joy](Joy.md) per wave survived and a prize of 16 [joy](Joy.md) on completion with a total payout of 40 [joy](Joy.md) on a full run
    -   Getting hit by a [Gourdskipper](Gourdskipper.md) makes you infected, each player you infect gives you an additional 3 [joy](Joy.md) before the wave gamemode is finished.
    -   You are unable to use mantras and equip items other than your currently equipped weapon, M1s are replaced with the [Pumpkin Pitch](Pumpkin_Pitch.md) [mantra](Mantras.md) but you are still able to use your critical attack (might be a bug or unintentional feature).
    -   The Trial is located inside the [Widow's Wail Cemetary](<Widow's_Wail_Cemetery.md>) (on your left after teleporting).
    -   You will get a small chest on wave 3, wave 5, wave 7, and a boss chest after completing wave 8 the final wave. Chests can include umbral obsidian, [hallowtide mask](Hallowtide_Mask.md) crafting ingredients, [deep gems](Deep_Gems.md), [goodie bags](Goodie_Bag.md), various hallowtide exclusive weapons, familiar armor sets, hallowtide exclusive cosmetics, and headgear.

## Trivia

-   The music for this area is [A Light In The Sky](A_Light_In_The_Sky.md) by Naktigonis
-   The combat music for this area is [A Darkness Beneath](Deepwoken_OST.md#a-darkness-beneath) by Naktigonis
-   Initially, there was a bug where the Void Feather cost 5 Joy instead of the required 15. This was patched quickly.
-   The Familiar Priest, Heretic and Knight helmet are direct references to their counterparts from _Rogue Lineage_, being the Church Knight Helmet, Dark Sigil Helmet and Sigil Helmet respectively.
-   Whenever you try to spook Glaive Spellhardt with a Hallowtide Mask, he doesn't get spooked and instead says: "Oh please."
-   This location appears in the Battle Royale mode as an "intermission" before the game starts, but there are no Lightborn NPCs
-   On October 17, 2023 there used to be a floating gate above the castle. However, it was presumably removed during one of the bug fix updates following the release of Hallowtide.
    -   For some reason, the floating gate is back in Hallowtide 2024, it's unknown if it will get removed yet.
-   There was a bugged version of the Floating Keep in certain servers where if someone with [Lightborn](Lightborn.md) in the [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md) joined in [Pathfinder](Pathfinder.md) it would spawn the Keep, only accessible by gliding from a high point or with Ad Astra. In this bugged state, some interactables, like the wells, stop working, and [Lysander Spellhardt](Lysander_Spellhardt.md) is replaced with one of [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md)'s [Chest Masters](Chest_Master.md). This was the only [Chest Master](Chest_Master.md) accessible outside of [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md).
-   Purchasing the [Weapon Manual](Weapon_Manual.md) at the Floating Keep causes it to stack with the player's pre-existing Weapon Manual in their inventory, although this has no real use case. This item has a stack limit of 25.
