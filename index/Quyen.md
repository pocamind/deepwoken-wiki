## Description

Quyen is an [Etrean](Etrean.md) NPC.

When interacted with, they request 6 [wood](Wood.md) in order to keep the fires lit. In exchange, [the Inheritor](The_Inheritor.md) gains 5 Notes.

Their counterpart in [Etris](<Etris_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) is [Linh](Linh.md).

## Location

Quyen is located on the [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md). They are standing beside a log next an empty hosue.
