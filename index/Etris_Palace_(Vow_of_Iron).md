## Overview

The Etris Palace was once home to the [Lord Guardian](Lord_Guardian.md) [Ji'ang](<Ji'ang.md>) before Zi'eer's coup resides in [Etris](<Etris_(Vow_of_Iron).md>), located in [The King's Garden](<The_King's_Garden.md>). It is surrounded by palace [Shogun of the Prophet's Guard](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>) on the inside and outside.

The palace has two floors, the first has some unremarkable rooms and Zi'eer's process of making [Shogun's](<Shogun_of_the_Prophet's_Guard.md>), and the second floor is where you will encounter [Zi'eer, the Fourth Prophet](<Zi'eer,_the_Fourth_Prophet.md>). Zi'eer will immediately pierce you on all sides before you are sent to [The Throne of Lies](The_Throne_of_Lies.md).
