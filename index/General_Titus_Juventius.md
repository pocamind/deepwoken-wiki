## Description

General Titus Juventius is a [boss](Bosses.md) fought at [The Polymarch](The_Polymarch.md) within the Eastern Luminant. He is the captain of his ship, commanding the Red Legion members, presumably the [49th Legion](The_Hundred_Legions.md), and a user of [Oath: Blightsurger](Oath%253A_Blightsurger.md), yet a bit weaker than known by his older form. This boss is optional, as he is merely a mission-based boss that only helps you gain [Notes](Notes.md) to power up. If Titus's HP reaches 90%, he forfeits and you unlock the [Theros Disciple](Theros_Disciple.md) [Memento](Mementos.md) and gives the player the [Icarus Sun Shield](Icarus_Sun_Shield.md) before warping away from the player if gone through his dialog.

A young, Silverback [Khan](Khan.md) with great stature, he's around twice the height of the player. His face forms a stern expression with bright yellow eyes, while donning a gold crown that sits atop his black dreads. He wears a dark blue [Legion Centurion](Legion_Centurion.md) with his [own cape](<Titus's_Cape.md>). Unlike his older version, he does not have the [Worldpiercer Gauntlets](Worldpiercer_Gauntlets.md) and has not unlocked his [Resonance](Resonance.md) yet, indicating that he still learns how to become stronger, though he should not be underestimated as he's still a tough foe to handle.

### Starting the Fight with Titus

1.  Talk to the [Reclaimer](Reclaimer_of_the_Recollection.md) at [The Cerebral Spire](The_Cerebral_Spire.md), take the mission/task option and interact if it says, "memory of a commander".
2.  Walk up to the five teleporters up the stairs and (from left to right) take the fourth teleporter to arrive at [The Polymarch](The_Polymarch.md).
3.  Recommended but not mandatory: Kill the [Legion Soldiers](<Legion_Soldier_(The_Polymarch).md>) to avoid getting attacked during the fight of the minions
4.  Interact with the [Site of a Bygone Memory](Site_of_a_Bygone_Memory.md) at the center of the ship and wait till Titus lands on it and start fighting him.

## Tips

-   As a general rule, do not overcommit to attacks. Focus on maintaining defense and punishing his attacks rather than forcing openings. Stay mobile and use mantras for ranged damage when necessary.
-   Make sure to not get hit by his Warp Kick as it may send you into the water making it much harder to fight him as some of his attacks require jumping.
-   It is generally better to avoid using your [Etris Flask](Etris_Flask.md) during the fight, as Titus deals [Wither](Status_Effects.md#wither) damage. His attacks are also very quick, and attempting to create distance to use the [Etris Flask](Etris_Flask.md) can be risky, as Titus may use a Warp Kick.
-   It's better to fight Titus in the lower deck of [The Polymarch](The_Polymarch.md) since his [Legion Soldiers](<Legion_Soldier_(The_Polymarch).md>) can spawn mid fight, and you have less of a chance of being sent flying off the ship.
-   If you are low on HP you can run into the [Boundary](<Boundary_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) in [The Polymarch](The_Polymarch.md) to regain some HP or you could wait out the [Combat Tag](Combat_Tag.md) to fully recover and try again, but Titus would of most likely have recovered himself.
-   It's possible to easily defeat Titus by luring him to a [whirlpool](Whirlpools.md). Titus will use Warp Kick, likely into the whirlpool's range, and thus be sucked in, instantly killing him. An easy way of doing this is being combat tagged by Titus and running into the [Boundary](<Boundary_(Vow_of_Iron).md>) then going to [Meteor Isle](Meteor_Isle.md), then get close enough to [The Polymarch](The_Polymarch.md) that Titus will Warp Kick to you and slowly getting closer to the [whirlpool](Whirlpools.md).
