## Description

The Harvestman's Halo is an **unenchantable**[\[1\]](#cite-note-unenchantable-1) [Equipment](Equipment.md) item under the "Head" category.

[Ardenstar Rage](Talents.md#ardenstar-rage) halves the cooldown all Mantras while you are below half health. When you are below 20% health, you will additionally apply [Cursed Burn](Status_Effects.md#cursed-burn) on all of your Mantras. Cursed Burn does 1.75x the damage of regular [Burn](Status_Effects.md#burn). You also constantly take damage while wearing the crown.

## Trivia

-   The [Gatherhand](Gatherhand.md) on the halo translates to: "At hallows tide, with reaping blade, O, Ardenstar, we call thy name."
-   The cooldown reduction effect stacks with the [Blessed Gem](Blessed_Gem.md), for 75% Mantra cooldown reduction.
    -   Pairing this with the [Brain Food Buff](Status_Effects.md#brain-food-buff) from the [Grilled Parasol Brain](Grilled_Parasol_Brain.md) will increase this to 83.5% cooldown reduction.
-   The Cursed Burn damage multiplier is applied after all other burn modifications, including additive bonuses.
