**Familiar Arachnid's Cloak** is a Unique [Outfit](Outfits.md) in _Deepwoken_.

## Statistics

### _Requirements_

This Outfit costs 5,000 and requires five (5) [Cloth](Cloth.md), five (5) [Fiber](Fiber.md), one (1) [Wayward Gem](Wayward_Gem.md), five (5) [Dark Feather](Dark_Feather.md), one (1) [Grim](Grim.md) Enchant Stone, and three (3) [Spider Egg](Spider_Egg.md) to craft This Outfit additionally requires the player to be at least [Power](Power.md) 15 to be crafted.

### _Armor Stats_

This Outfit has 3500 durability and grants +20% Physical resistance ResistancePhysical, +15% Elemental resistance [ResistanceElemental](Attunements.md), +10% Shadow Armor [ResistanceShadowcast](Shadowcast.md), and +40% Ether Regen [IconEther](Ether.md). Additionally, it provides the [Arachnid Surge](Talents.md#arachnid-surge) Talent.

-   Because related resistances from non-[Equipment](Equipment.md) stat sources are combined multiplicatively, and not additively, the total resistances are: 20% Physical ResistancePhysical, 23.5% Shadowcast [ResistanceShadowcast](Shadowcast.md), 15% Elemental [ResistanceBloodrend](Bloodrend.md) [ResistanceFlamecharm](Flamecharm.md) [ResistanceFrostdraw](Frostdraw.md) [ResistanceGalebreathe](Galebreathe.md) [ResistanceIronsing](Ironsing.md) [ResistanceThundercall](Thundercall.md)..

The [Arachnid Surge](Talents.md#arachnid-surge) Talent grants a Talent tool that upon activation gives you a brief window of very powerful climbing, at the cost of wearing out your ankles. This gives you a heavy slow after Arachnid Surge ends.

## Trivia

-   This outfit is based on Rogue's Lineage's [Spider Cloak](https://rogue-lineage.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_Cloak) Armor.
-   In Rogue Lineage, the Spider Cloak is an artifact that replaces your armor with the Spider Cloak. While very squishy, it provides unparalleled mobility, notably having the highest climb speed multiplier in the game.
-   Side walljumps do not get buffed.
-   The user is still vulnerable to air counters.
