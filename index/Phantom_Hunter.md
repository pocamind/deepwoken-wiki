## Description

The **Phantom Hunter** is a Mini Boss exclusive to [Vow of Iron](Vow_of_Iron.md). They have a chance to appear when igniting a [Sanguine Candle](Sanguine_Candle.md) or on completion of any [job](Job_Boards.md).
