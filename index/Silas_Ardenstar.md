## Description

Silas Ardenstar was a [Lightborn](Lightborn.md) and head of the [Ardenstar family](Ardenstar_family.md), who sought immortality through getting rid of his humanity. He somehow distilled the true Ichor from his veins and he was a step closer to his immortality, but he died at some point in his pursuit.[\[2\]](#cite-note-2) Silas was embalmed and laid to rest.[\[1\]](#cite-note-arden-1)

At some point the [Spellhardt family](Spellhardt_family.md) thought it would be funny to make his enormous tombstone into a [greatsword](<Silas's_Cenotaph.md>), and seemingly also exhumed his body and severed his head.[\[1\]](#cite-note-arden-1)
