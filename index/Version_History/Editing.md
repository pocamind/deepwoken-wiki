# Version History/Editing

## Intro

Editing Version History may seem like a difficult task, but it is actually a relatively straightforward process. This utilizes the website [Find and Replace Online](https://textcleaner.net/find-and-replace/) which is hosted by textcleaner. You are just copying the update logs into Find and Replace and then doing some essential formatting. Lets take [this](https://discord.com/channels/920707638034067486/920709120548544564/1531072591760982162) update from July 26, 2026 as an example.

## Step 2: Formatting

The formatting is the most important part of the process. It utilizes 5 important text structures:

-   '''<h2>Month Day, Year; pv\_XXX\_XX\_XX:XXa'''</h2><div class="mw-collapsible mw-collapsed">
-   <br>
-   '''
-   <h3><u>'''''Patch X.X.Xa; pv\_XXX\_XX\_XX:XXa'''''</u></h3>
-   </div>

  
We first start off with the '''<h2>Month Day, Year; pv\_XXX\_XX\_XX:XXa'''</h2><div class="mw-collapsible mw-collapsed">, and place it at the very beginning of the text right before Job Improvements & Quality of Life. Since this update was posted on July 26th, 2026 at 22:55 UTC with the ingame update for that date being pv\_JUL\_27\_01:33a, the updated beginning text would turn into <h2>'''July 26th, 2026; pv\_JUL\_27\_01:33a'''</h2><div class="mw-collapsible mw-collapsed">.  
  
For the second step of formatting, we put <br> and ''' in their correct places. For every Heading text like Job Improvements & Quality of Life, Authority Ensign Improvements, and Fishing Improvements, we place three apostrophes before and after the word like this: '''Job Improvements & Quality of Life''', '''Authority Ensign Improvements''', and '''Fishing Improvements'''. Then, for every heading that is not the first one of the update, we add a <br> before the triple apostrophes. It would look like this: '''Job Improvements & Quality of Life''', <br>'''Authority Ensign Improvements''', and <br>'''Fishing Improvements'''.  
  
Once this has been completed for all the sections of update, we can place the </div> at the very end of the update like so: 🩹 Fixed item alignment on Western & Ascended Outlaw Breeches</div>. Additionally, there are sometimes instances where certain words are _italicized_ within the update logs i.e ⏫ Your void threshold now adjusts instantly when _gaining_ combat tags. Losing tags still has it gradually adjust, keeping the prevention of 'mb all' abuse. When this happens, just simply put a '' before and after the word to italicize it. Note that italicized text does not copy over automatically to Find and Replace, so you will have to manually keep a look out for it. Finally, we place a </div> at the end of the text.
