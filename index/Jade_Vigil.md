# Jade Vigil

| Jade Vigil | |
|---|---|
| territory | Isle of Vigils |

## Description

Jade Vigils are a rank unique to the who have mastered the technique of [Fang and Coil](Fang_and_Coil.md).[\[1\]](#cite-note-1) Upon learning the technique they don the [Jade Vigil's Weave](<Jade_Vigil's_Weave.md>) and are granted use of the [Steelscale Dusters](Steelscale_Dusters.md).[\[2\]](#cite-note-2)
