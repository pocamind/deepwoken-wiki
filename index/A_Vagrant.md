## Trivia

-   They are hostile to every NPC, including the [Teacher](Teacher.md) NPC.
-   They wear the [Stranded](Stranded.md) outfit.
-   In previous versions, they were known as Forgotten Villagers, and yielded a set of dialogue that would trigger an event consisting of two enemy waves.
