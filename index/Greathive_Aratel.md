# Greathive Aratel

LocationL Greathive Aratel LocationR

TERRITORY OF The Hive

Life in all its variety hums around you. The immensity of what has been accomplished in cultivating this place washes over you.

  

**Greathive Aratel** is a location in _Deepwoken_. It is a settlement in the [Aratel Sea](The_Aratel_Sea.md).

## Overview

Greathive Aratel is a city built in a giant tree on the sea. Inside this tree is a large settlement: the Greathive itself.

To enter Greathive Aratel, you must have:

-   washed your face (requires you to be at least [Power](Power.md) 10 to wash your face) on any character or slot, and
-   have visited the [Eastern Luminant](Luminants.md#etrean-and-eastern-luminants) at least once on your current character.

Alternatively, you can [sail](Ships_&_Sailing.md) to Greathive Aratel, but getting to the top requires mobility mantras, clever usage of [gliders](Gliders.md), lots of parkour and jumping on the heads of players. If you washed your face and have already invoked the Greathive fountain found in The Lordsgrove, you can sail to the shipyard of the Greathive Aratel and use the fountain in there in order to get teleported up to the entrance floor of Greathive Aratel.

## Contents

Greathive Aratel is split up into 4 floors:

-   [First floor](#first-floor)
-   [Root Plaza](#root-plaza)
-   [Heart Plaza](#heart-plaza)
-   [Canopy Plaza](#canopy-plaza)

### First Floor

#### Greathive Fountains

-   There is a Heat Stone resting spot and Fountains functioning as fast travel.

-   Five of [The Hive](The_Hive.md)'s fountains can be found within Greathive Aratel.
    -   The left fountain leads to [Summer Isle](Summer_Isle.md).
    -   The middle-left fountain leads to [Miner's Landing](<Miner's_Landing.md>).
    -   The center fountain leads back to [the Lordsgrove](The_Lordsgrove.md).
    -   The middle-right fountain leads to [Ancient Rotlands](Ancient_Rotlands.md).
    -   The right fountain leads to [The Temple of the Forgotten Flame](The_Temple_of_the_Forgotten_Flame.md).
        -   Fountains have a 30-second cooldown, unless you are teleporting from the [Etrean Luminant](Luminants.md#etrean-and-eastern-luminants), in which case no initial cooldown is applied.
-   Several fountains throughout the [Etrean Luminant](Luminants.md#etrean-and-eastern-luminants) can be used to return to Greathive Aratel:
    -   Inside [The Hidden Village](The_Hidden_Village.md).
    -   Within [The King's Garden](<The_King's_Garden.md>), to the left of [Etris Palace](Etris_Palace.md).
    -   At [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md), opposite the path leading to [Friid](Friid,_Master_of_Gale.md), inside the ruins beside a tree.
    -   At [The Lordsgrove](The_Lordsgrove.md).
    -   The fountain in [The Lordsgrove](The_Lordsgrove.md) is the largest of all Hive teleporters.

#### Shipyard

-   In the entrance floor of the Greathive Aratel and to the left, the player can find a Shipyard fountain that will spawn the player down to the shipyard area, In the shipyard the player can spawn, craft and recall their [Ships](Ships_&_Sailing.md).
    -   If the player has ally [Reputation](Reputation.md) with [the Hive](The_Hive.md), the [Shipwright](Shipwright.md) [Kaab](Kaab.md) will bring up ship modification, or [Lifeweaveing](Lifeweave.md).
    -   The Shipwright NPC will not spawn, repair or recall the player's ship until the player reaches ally [Reputation](Reputation.md) with [The Hive](The_Hive.md).

#### Library

-   [Gear Cabinet](Training_Gear.md) to buy training equipment.
-   There is a [Mantra Lectern](Mantra_Lectern.md) near the entrance.
-   [Hiri](Hiri.md) and [Liviu](Liviu.md) can be found here mostly standing around.

### Root Plaza

#### Cargo

-   A corridor of two rooms.
-   At the end is a door that can be broken that leads to outside the Greathive.
-   The room to the left contains [Vinegar](Vinegar.md).
-   The room to the right contains the [Banker](Banker.md).

#### Archive

-   Attunementless Mantra Trainer [Regek](Regek.md).
-   A [Barber](Barber.md) at the balcony.

#### Antiquarian

-   Players can find an [Antiquarian](Antiquarian.md) who will purchase their sellable items.
-   [Cloth](Cloth.md) is sold here for 6 each.
-   [Fiber](Fiber.md) is sold here for 3 each.

### Heart Plaza

#### R&D

-   [Laplace](Laplace.md), the enchanter resides in this room. Hand in **three** enchanted items ([Weapons](Weapons.md) and [Equipment](Equipment.md)) to enchant your currently equipped weapon, provided it is eligible. You can also purchase [Enchant Grease](Enchant_Grease.md) from Laplace for 5, allowing the user to apply an enchant to their Equipment.
-   Behind the central metal bookshelf in the main R&D room, there are two breakable walls, which if destroyed by a strong enough mantra, reveals a hidden room with a phonograph playing "[A Memory of Tides](Deepwoken_OST.md#a-memory-of-tides)" by Naktigonis.
-   Using any other environment-destroying mantra on the wooden bookshelf in the right hallway a few times will reveal a, now presumably unused, extra room with three "containers" and broken pipes leaking gas.

### Canopy Plaza

Hidden within the tree is another Heat Stone resting spot and nearbly is a pool of, tree sap that can be used to fill up [Canteen](Canteen.md).

#### Blacksmith

-   Players can find a [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md).
-   Players are able to purchase a [Pickaxe](Pickaxe.md) and [Lumber Axe](Lumber_Axe.md) for 3 each.
-   A [Crafting Station](Crafting_Station.md) is found right next to the Blacksmith.

#### Inn

-   Players can spend 5 to set their spawn point at Greathive Aratel.

### Canopy Peak

-   The office of the current Hivelord. Features a [Hive Guard](Hive_Guard.md) and a mere statue of the most powerful being in Lumen, [Destroyman III](Destroyman_III.md).

#### Observatory

-   Greathive Aratel is home to the Celestial Telescope, which offers players a glimpse into the [Lore](Glossary.md) surrounding [Constellia](Constellia.md) and the [Celestials](Celestial.md).
    -   [Instruments](Instruments.md) can also be purchased here.
    -   When turning the crank you will be shown these images depending on which one you turn

## Quests

### Grave Tending

-   By going and talking to [Suri](Suri.md). He will give you a flower, and a task to go and place it at the grave of his brother on the [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md). The flower can be destroyed by taking any kind of damage.
-   If you come back to [Suri](Suri.md) after completing this quest, you get a [Vibrant Gem](Mantra_Modifiers.md#regular-modifiers).

### Blooming Souls Quest (Soul Flower Quest)

-   Talk to the NPC named [Regek](Regek.md), in a small library room, hey will tell you to fetch 3 Souls from special flowers that bloom within the Greathive, all of which located in the Canopy Plaza. They are called Soulblooms. (You need to wash your face at the temple to be able to take the quest)
    -   A red flower located outside near the observatory balcony. Mind your footing.
    -   A white flower, located near the [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md), facing in front of it.
    -   A blue flower, hanging at the side of a tree root across the room from the Blacksmith.
-   Completing this quest gives a handful of EXP and access to upgrade [Attunement-less](Attunement-less.md) Mantras on every character.

## Trivia

-   The music for this area is [Of Chitin, Kith And Kin](Deepwoken_OST.md#of-chitin-kith-and-kin) by Naktigonis.
-   The combat music for this area is [A Symphony Of Strife](Deepwoken_OST.md#a-symphony-of-strife) by Naktigonis.
-   The description of this area was previously, "The stirring of life of all kinds fills the air as you enter the shade of the canopy."
-   Parts of the Greathive can be seen emitting black particles, which is fireproofing that allows [Flamecharm](Flamecharm.md) to be used within the Greathive without the whole thing going up in flames.
-   The Starswept Valley is only dead because all nutrients of the lush forest were sucked up to nourish the growing Greathive.
-   Everyone inside Greathive Aratel is a [Hivekin](Hivekin.md): either a [Vesperian](Vesperian.md), [Ganymede](Ganymede.md), or [Chrysid](Chrysid.md).
-   Artifacts are able to spawn at Hive. However, originally, [Bankers](Banker.md) did not exist in Aratel meaning they couldn't be made use of, without travelling to either [Fort Merit](Fort_Merit.md) or [Miner's Landing](<Miner's_Landing.md>), for over two years.
-   According to [Destroyman III](Destroyman_III.md), Greathive Aratel makes the best soft shell tacos in the entire Luminant.
-   Back in [Verse 1](Verse_1.md), there was only three Teleporters instead of five.
