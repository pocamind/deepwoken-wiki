# Tomb of the Nameless Warrior

**Tomb of the Nameless Warrior** is a location in _Deepwoken_. It is a landmark below the [Burning Stone Gardens](Burning_Stone_Gardens.md) found in [Upper Erisia](Upper_Erisia.md).

## Overview

Tomb of the Nameless Warrior is a cavern that is home to a single [Rotskipper](Rotskipper.md). A hidden door behind the [Rotskipper](Rotskipper.md) can be broken open with any destructible Mantra.

The entrance can be found on a shore around the rear of [Erisia](Erisia.md).

## Contents

### The Obelisk

-   Under the tomb itself is a ladder that leads to a large glowing obelisk.

## Trivia

-   Most likely a reference to the "Tomb of the Unknown Soldier" in Washington D.C.
