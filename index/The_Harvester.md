## Description

The Harvester is a [Boss](Bosses.md) encountered during [Hallowtide](Hallowtide.md) World Events. It is a towering scarecrow wearing a long, ragged black robe tied together by a red cloth, alongside a cloak with pumpkin shoulderpads and numerous layers of tattered cloth. The Harvester's head is a pile of straw emanating an orange glow, while having a radiating yellow orb at floating above it. It also has a second pair of mechanical arms that are primarily used in combat.
