# Caudal Glade

LocationL Caudal Glade LocationR

A remote clearing atop the Spine sometimes utilised by the Vigils as a ground for training and mock exercises

  

## Description

The Caudal Glade is a sparring grounds area at the edge of the [Spine of the East](Spine_of_the_East.md). It is located behind the [Isle of Vigils](Isle_of_Vigils.md) and atop the furthest sea gate.

## Points of Interest

### Sparring Ground

-   Campfires can be found sparsely spread around the large field.

## Trivia

-   This area is notoriously known for being a hotspot for [Guilds](Guilds.md) to fight eachother.
    -   It has been used in many officially hosted competitions for its massive open space.
