## Description

The Evanspear War Helmet is an [Equipment](Equipment.md) item under the "Head" category, and is a part of the Evanspear War Set. The Evanspear War Helmet is a green and yellow corinthian helmet, similar to Roman headgear.

This item is used to craft the [Reinforced War Helmet](Reinforced_War_Helmet.md) at the [Merit Detainment Core](Merit_Detainment_Core.md).

## Recipes

After defeating [Titus](Titus_Fulminus_Destellus.md), a crafting bench within the [Merit Detainment Core](Merit_Detainment_Core.md) will be open to you. This item's recipes are only craftable with that bench. The recipes are as follows:

-   [Reinforced War Helmet](Reinforced_War_Helmet.md):
    1.  1x Evanspear War Helmet, 1x [Warden's Armor Piece](<Warden's_Armor_Piece.md>), and 2x [Wrath Gems](Wrath_Gem.md).
    2.  1x Evanspear War Helmet, 1x [Primadon Pelt](Primadon_Pelt.md), and 2x [Wrath Gems](Wrath_Gem.md).
    3.  1x Evanspear War Helmet, 1x [Doom of Caeranthil's Scale](<Doom_of_Caeranthil's_Scale.md>), and 2x [Wrath Gems](Wrath_Gem.md).
    4.  1x Evanspear War Helmet, 1x [Titus' Armor Piece](<Titus'_Armor_Piece.md>), and 2x [Wrath Gems](Wrath_Gem.md).
