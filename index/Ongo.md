## Description

**Ongo** is a [Food](Food.md) item and crafting ingredient.

It is a type of **Generic** food.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 8% Stomach and 6% Water.

## Crafting

### Used in

-   [Candied Fruit](Candied_Fruit.md) - 2 Ongos (or any other [Fruits](Ingredients.md#fruits)) and 1 [Sap](Sap.md) at a [Campfire](Campfire_Pit.md) [CraftingCampfire](Campfire_Pit.md).
-   [Fruit Cake](Fruit_Cake.md) - 2 Ongos (or any other [Fruits](Ingredients.md#fruits)) and 1 [Gathered Wheat](Gathered_Wheat.md) at a [Campfire](Campfire_Pit.md) [CraftingCampfire](Campfire_Pit.md).
-   [Fruit Salad](Fruit_Salad.md) - 1 Ongo and 2 other [Fruits](Ingredients.md#fruits) in your inventory.
-   [Kyrsdog](Kyrsdog.md) - 1 Ongo, 1 [Bread](Bread.md), and 1 [Frozen Membrane](Frozen_Membrane.md) at a [Campfire](Campfire_Pit.md) [CraftingCampfire](Campfire_Pit.md).

## Trivia

-   An Ongo is a real fruit that comes from the Myrianthus arboreus tree, native to tropical Africa, ranging from Guinea to Tanzania. It is prized for its sweet, tangy pulp and nutritional benefits.
