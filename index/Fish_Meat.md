## Description

**Fish Meat** is a craftable [Food](Food.md) item and crafting ingredient.

### Effect

When consumed, it replenishes 5% Stomach and 3% Water.

## Crafting

### Recipe

-   Any Fish. Different types of fish will craft into varying amounts of Fish Meat:
    -   Sea Bass crafts into 1.
    -   Red Snapper crafts into 2.
    -   Tuna crafts into 3.
    -   Swordfish crafts into 4.
