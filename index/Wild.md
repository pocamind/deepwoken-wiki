## Description

Wild is a damage dealing [enchantment](Enchantments.md) that applies deals your weapon's damage in an AoE.

## Effect

On weapon hit, Wild deals 85% of your weapon's damage in an ~18.25 stud AoE around the target you hit.

Allies take 50% of your weapon's damage from Wild instead of the full 85%.

### Notes

-   Wild's damage is dealt as typeless damage, bypassing armor resistances.
-   Wild's AoE can only proc once per weapon attack; even if you hit multiple enemies with the same attack, Wild will only proc once.
-   Wild cannot hit the target who was hit by the initial attack, unless two or more targets were hit at the same time. If this happens, the first target will only take the damage from the initial attack, while all subsequent targets will take the initial attack's damage _and_ (a singular instance of) Wild's damage.
-   Wild's AoE damage can be parried and blocked, dealing 0 posture damage if blocked.
-   Attacking player summons or NPCs is also a great way to chain damage to your opponent.
-   Like all other effects that trigger on scaled damage, using Wild with the [Poser's Ring](<Poser's_Ring.md>), or any other source of the [Stored Damage Mechanic](Stored_Damage_Mechanic.md), will cause nearby targets to take Wild's AoE damage, while storing the damage dealt to the initial target. This is extremely useful when utilizing allies or allied NPCs as conduits for Wild's AoE damage, as they will take no damage until the user uses the Suspended Hit.
