# Mounts

| Mounts | |
|---|---|

**[Pathfinder](Pathfinder.md) Exclusive**  
This page describes content that is exclusive to the **Pathfinder** gamemode.  
This content is not accessible outside of this gamemode.

## Description

Mounts are tamed creatures or objects that can be used to traverse terrain quicker. Obtaining a mount will give you the [Beast Whistle](Beast_Whistle.md), which is used to summon your mount. However, equipping a [Weapon](Weapons.md), using a [Mantra](Mantras.md), or getting [Combat Tagged](Combat_Tag.md) will instantly dismount you, and you cannot summon your mount while in combat.

## Mount Types

### Thresher

-   _**Obtainment:**_ The Thresher mount can be purchased at [Sordino](Sordino.md) after completing his quest. Doing so will unlock his shop permanently across all slots for 3.
    -   The Thresher walks with speed of 22 stud/sec and runs with speed of 34.5 stud/s.
    -   The Thresher mount's movement speed is affected by any movement speed modifiers you currently have, though the effectiveness of your movement speed modifiers will be greatly reduced.

### Felwind Broom

-   _**Obtainment:**_ The Felwind Broom mount can be purchased at [Carmilla Spellhardt](Carmilla_Spellhardt.md) in [The Floating Keep](The_Floating_Keep.md) for 1000 joy. Once you had bought it from Carmilla, this mount will be unlocked permanently across all slots at [Sordino](Sordino.md) for a price of 15.
    -   Using the critical while mounted will cause the broom to do a dash that costs 50 flat [Ether](Ether.md).
    -   The critical will go the same distance, even in water.

## Trivia

-   Mounts can walk on lava without dismounting the rider.
-   If you are carrying something before using the Whistle, you will continue to carry said item while mounted. This greatly increases your movement speed while carrying objects.
-   Having a mount doesn't negate fall damage but slightly reduces it by around 2%.
-   With a mount you can completely resist the slow effect of the wind in the [Second Layer](Second_Layer.md), but still can be pushed away.
