**The Complete Map of the Eastern Luminant** is a fully printed cardstock poster that takes after the entire Eastern Luminant.

Every dwindling spit of land and treacherous stretch of sea within the Eastern Luminant has been brought to life in this gorgeously hand-illustrated poster by the artist. Whether it's hanging on your wall at home or in your guild base, it'll serve as an essential reference to chart your guild's next voyage.
