## Description

**The Complete Map of the Eastern Luminant** is a fully printed cardstock poster that takes after the entire Eastern Luminant.

### Official Description

Every dwindling spit of land and treacherous stretch of sea within the Eastern Luminant has been brought to life in this gorgeously  
hand-illustrated poster by the artist. Whether it's hanging on your wall at home or in your guild base, it'll serve as an essential reference to chart your guild's next voyage.  
Hand-drawn by real artists in collaboration with the Deepwoken  
team.  
Purchase the bundle in-game to unlock your guild base map instantly, with the physical poster shipped directly to you.  
Printed on heavyweight 100lb text matte cardstock. Your character might get ganked, but your poster shall live  
on.
