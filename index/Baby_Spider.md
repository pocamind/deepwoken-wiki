## **Description**

Long thought cut content the baby widow made its return in 25' [Hallowtide](Hallowtide.md) via [The Weaving Webs](The_Weaving_Web.md) critical attack, the Baby Spider shares all but the Deep webbing attacks of the [Deep Widow](Deep_Widow.md).

Physical attacks deal 40% less damage and its attacks may give sanity damage.
