## Description

The Alloyblood Daggers are one of three Alloyblood Weapons and is a [Dagger](Daggers.md) in the Light Weapons category. Conjured from the Alloyblood itself, this weapon boasts a hastened swing speed, with each attack applying a dibilitating effect on hit ([see below](#talents)).

This weapon can only be obtained by using the [Infused Slash](Hybrid_Abilities.md#infused-slash) Alloyblood Mantra after building up enough [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) on the target via [Bloodrend](Bloodrend.md) and [Ironsing](Ironsing.md) attacks. This weapon has a set duration after the Mantra is used.

## Alloyblood Upgrades

This weapon can be further enhanced through the [Composite Path: Alloyblood](Talents.md#composite-path-alloyblood) Talents:

-   [Stored Plasma](Talents.md#stored-plasma) and [Retained Hemoglobin](Talents.md#retained-hemoglobin) increase the duration of the Alloyblood Daggers, after the player feints or casts a different Alloyblood Weapon Mantra, respectively.
-   [Ultrafiltration](Talents.md#ultrafiltration) allows the weapon attacks on Alloyblood Daggers to apply the [Metal Rods](Status_Effects.md#metal-rods) and [Blood Poison](Status_Effects.md#blood-poison) status effects. These status effects are normally replaced with [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) with this Attunement Path, so this is very impactful.
-   [Plasma Refresh](Talents.md#plasma-refresh) refreshes your critical attack cooldown upon using an Alloyblood Mantra, allowing you to use your weapon critical again immediately after swapping to the Alloyblood weapon. Additionally, it allows the Alloyblood weapons to apply [Composite Alloy](Status_Effects.md#composite-alloy) buildup on critical attacks.

## Talents

Equipping the Alloyblood Daggers grants its unique [weapon Talent](Talents.md#weapon-talents)(s). Weapon Talents will be lost upon equipping any other weapon, including ability-bound weapons, like from the [Crazy Slots](Crazy_Slots.md) Resonance.

[**Quick Incisions**](Talents.md#quick-incisions) allows each alternating light attack of the daggers to apply a different elemental effect from Ironsing and Bloodrend. Left-handed attacks apply [Blood Poison](Status_Effects.md#blood-poison), while right-handed attacks steal [armor durability](Outfits.md).

## Critical

Alloyblood Daggers uses the default Dagger critical, a series of 3 quick slashes. The first hit has a scaled damage multiplier of **1x**, while the second and third have a scaled damage multiplier of **0.8x**. It has a cooldown of **3 seconds**.

## Trivia

-   This weapon retains the [Enchantment](Enchantments.md) and quality star bonus as your main-hand weapon.
